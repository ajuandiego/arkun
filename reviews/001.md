## Verdict

The hunt, the first touch, and the Redoubt treason are already a book. Sector 09 has a throat, a price for air, and two bodies that feel dangerous in the same room. What threatens the book is that the ending tells the opening's secret again, then tells a second ending in which Vram has vanished and Tsunari wakes branded. Alongside that, the romance arrives as full intercourse in the Barrens and again in the Vault, while two canon files still disagree about whether Book 1 is allowed to do that at all. Fix the doubled revelation and the doubled ending before another prose pass.
