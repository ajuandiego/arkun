# The Bodkin

> **Format:** Ready-to-copy prompts for Gemini / Imagen.  
> **Style:** The same comic sheet as the character plates: bold ink, crosshatch, halftone. No portrait. The point is the machine on a body.  
> **Rule:** These plates follow the canon. The leash is one socket at the nape and two links down the upper back. The Bodkin is one dark knife until it is taken apart. Then the blade and the pointed pommel both come off, and the hidden contacts show at both ends of the grip. A glowing gadget, a battery, or a radio in the grip is wrong.


Match `assets/media/bodkin.jpg`. Seated, it is one dark knife. Apart, the hidden contacts show from both ends.

*   **Seated:** a five-inch dark carbon-steel blade, a slim engraved grip, a bright collar where the blade meets the grip, and a faceted pointed pommel. No visible tech.
*   **Apart:** the blade is off one end. The pointed pommel is off the other. Both ends of the grip show dark metal interface contacts. No glow.

### Prompt
```text
Hardware study in modern western comic book art style, bold ink lineart, crosshatching and halftone dot shading, crisp colors. Plain warm-gray ground. No text, no captions, no labels, no letters, no numbers. No person. No hand. No hair.

Two views of the same small knife, drawn large. The whole object, seated, is about the length of a hand. Not a sword.

Left, seated: one dark piece. A five-inch straight carbon-steel blade with a sharp point and a real edge. A slim dark grip with faint engraved lines. A narrow bright metal collar where the blade meets the grip. A faceted pointed pommel at the butt, like a small steel spear-butt, not a flat cap. No seam that reads as a gadget. No glow. No wires. No screen.

Right, taken apart, the same three pieces laid in a row: the blade, the grip, the pointed pommel. The blade is off one end of the grip. The pointed pommel is off the other end. Both exposed ends of the grip show small dark metal interface contacts, rectangular nodes, not a circuit board and not a light. The blade's tang shows the matching contacts. The inside of the pommel is plain metal. No lead clipped on. No glow.

Forbidden: glow, LED, screen, battery, antenna, lightning, a USB stick, a gun, a person, text.
```
