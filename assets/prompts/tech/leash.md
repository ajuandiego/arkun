# The Leash on any chimera

> **Format:** Ready-to-copy prompts for Gemini / Imagen.  
> **Style:** The same comic sheet as the character plates: bold ink, crosshatch, halftone. No portrait. The point is the machine on a body.  
> **Rule:** These plates follow the canon. The leash is one socket at the nape and two links down the upper back. The Bodkin is one dark knife until it is taken apart. Then the blade and the pointed pommel both come off, and the hidden contacts show at both ends of the grip. A glowing gadget, a battery, or a radio in the grip is wrong.


The same hardware sits on every corporate chimera. Strain changes the body around it. It does not change the socket.

*   **Idle:** one round iris in a dark seat about three inches by three, centered where the head meets the back. Two link plates, each about two inches by two, follow the spine down one under the other. The flesh on either side of the socket lies flat.
*   **Hot:** the carrier is up. The flesh on the left and right sides of the socket is only slightly puffed. The neck stays a normal neck. The links stay metal. They do not swell. The locked plate is `assets/media/the_leash_2.jpg`.

### Prompt
```text
Character hardware sheet in modern western comic book art style, bold ink lineart, crosshatching and halftone dot shading, crisp colors. Plain warm-gray ground. No text, no captions, no labels, no letters, no numbers. No wings. No feathers.

Two views of the same generic adult, seen from behind, hair cropped short so the nape is bare. Neutral skin. No scales, no fur. Frame from the crown to the mid-back. Left view and right view only.

The leash, identical in both views:

1. One dark metal seat about three inches by three, centered where the head meets the back. A round iris port in the middle of that seat. One socket only.

2. Exactly two flat link plates, one under the other, leaving the bottom of that seat and following the spine down. Each plate is about two inches by two. Count them: an upper link and a lower link. Not side by side. Not a chain. Not one cable. Not a rod down the spine. Not jewelry.

Left view, idle: skin on both sides of the socket is flat and normal.

Right view, hot: the same socket and the same two links. The skin immediately left of the socket and immediately right of the socket is only slightly puffed, a mild swelling. The neck is still a normal neck. Not a lump. Not a goiter. The links are not swollen.

Forbidden: wings, feathers, a third figure, a chain, one single cable, a spine rod, a second port, a collar, a gun, text.
```
