# Human Chimeric Mutations: Resting vs. Enhanced State Codex

> **In-World Lore:** These anatomical plates represent clandestine medical dissections and physiological field studies authored by **Dr. Tsunari Thorne** and preserved in **The Glass Vault**. They illustrate the cellular, anatomical, and visual consequences of splicing human subjects with the 5 primordial beast lineages—contrasting both **Male and Female human bodies** in their **Resting State (Passive/Civilian Mode)** versus their **Enhanced State (Fight-or-Flight / Awakened Combat Mode)**.  
> **Format:** Ready-to-copy prompts optimized for Gemini / Imagen 3.  
> **Style Anchor:** Leonardo da Vinci’s *Vitruvian Man* and anatomical comparative studies, executed with graphite pencil and sepia iron-gall ink on aged parchment with manual drafting compass arcs, proportion guidelines, and selective watercolor tinting.

---

## 1. The Dual Biological States

Every augmented chimeric human exists in two distinct physiological modes:

```
[ RESTING STATE: DORMANT / CIVILIAN ]
       ↓ (Adrenaline Spike / Threat Activation / Arkun Surge)
[ ENHANCED STATE: FIGHT-OR-FLIGHT / AWAKENED COMBAT ]
```

### 1. The Resting State (Passive / Civilian Mode)
*   **Purpose:** Energy conservation and social integration inside the Green Domes and Gray Ring.
*   **Visual Manifestation:** 85–90% human appearance. Chimeric weaponry (claws, talons, spurs) sits completely retracted beneath nail beds or foot pads. Feather shafts and dermal scutes lie smooth and flat against the skin like silk ribbons. Capillary heat lines are dormant, and pupils retain a calm, controlled focus.

### 2. The Enhanced State (Fight-or-Flight / Awakened Combat Mode)
*   **Purpose:** Maximum kinetic lethality, supersonic speed, structural invulnerability, and instinctive pack survival.
*   **Visual Manifestation:** Full biological deployment. Claws snap forward; vascular tracks blaze with metabolic heat or bio-electric glow; plumage or dermal stone scutes bristle into aggressive threat displays and thermal radiators; pupils contract to predatory slits, and a clear eye membrane sweeps the eye.

## 2. Shared Prompt Architecture for Gemini

Each prompt in this directory follows a strict comparative format:
*   **Both Male and Female figures** are illustrated on the same manuscript page in a dual Vitruvian layout.
*   The **left side** depicts the subject in the **Resting State** (relaxed posture, retracted bio-traits, subtle tells).
*   The **right side** depicts the subject in the **Enhanced State** (dynamic combat stance, fully deployed claws/talons/wings, flared threat displays, glowing vascular tracks).
*   **Technical insets** detail the specific mechanical transitions (e.g., weapon strike angles, the heat output of capillary veins, or the interlocking of stone dermal plates).
