# Elder Gideon Cross (The Arch-Curator of Sector 09)

> **Format:** Ready-to-copy prompts optimized for Gemini / Imagen 3.  
> **Style Anchor:** Leonardo da Vinci character studies and anatomical portraiture on aged parchment, sepia ink cross-hatching, geometric proportion guides, and delicate watercolor tinting.


### Prompt
```text
A contemplative Renaissance portrait study in the style of Leonardo da Vinci or Rembrandt, depicting Elder Gideon Cross (age 67), Chief Bio-Curator of the Glass Vault, drawn on antique fibrous parchment.

The drawing portrays an elderly, dignified scholar-historian with deep, weathered character lines across his forehead and around his piercing, intelligent eyes. He wears a brass-framed optical magnifying loupe over one eye. Draped in heavy wool and layered linen curator robes with reinforced leather pockets holding calipers and drafting dividers. His hands are depicted in careful anatomical detail, holding an open circular optical quartz disc spool that catches the light with subtle, pale-blue watercolor tinting.

Behind him, lightly sketched drafting tables feature rolled parchment codices, inkwells, and brass astrolabes. Framed by delicate pencil guidelines, proportional facial geometry lines, and classical cursive archival annotations. Warm sepia ink, soft graphite shading, authentic aged paper texture with deckled edges and foxing. Scholarly, timeless, and deeply atmospheric.
```

*   **Aspect Ratio:** `3:4` or `4:3` (Portrait)
*   **Recommended Negatives:** `cartoon wizard, modern scientist in lab coat, digital painting, smooth 3D render, saturated vibrant colors`
