### Prompt

Manual drafting annotations cover the parchment: brass compass navigation arcs radiating from Sector 09, elevation contour lines sketched with light pencil strokes, scale calipers measuring leagues and kilometers, and handwritten cursive survival notes in the margins ('Respirator required beyond marker 4', 'Dromaeon pack stalking grounds in basalt ruins'). Aged paper with light tea stains, creased folds, and authentic Da Vinci notebook texture. High detail, classical hand-drawn cartography.
