# The Glass Vault (Subterranean Codex Schematic)

> **Format:** Ready-to-copy prompts optimized for Gemini / Imagen 3.  
> **Style Anchor:** Leonardo da Vinci architectural study, anatomical cutaway drawings on aged parchment, brass ruler guides, compass arcs, and selective watercolor washes.


### Prompt
```text
A Renaissance architectural isometric cutaway diagram of 'The Glass Vault' archive, eighty feet beneath Sector 09, drawn on heavily aged, sepia-toned parchment paper with manual drafting instruments.

The drawing reveals a multi-level subterranean sanctuary carved out of reinforced municipal subway bedrock. The centerpiece is the 'Great Hall of Quartz': towering circular tiers lined with thousands of illuminated optical quartz and synthetic sapphire disc spools, softly tinted in faint pale-blue watercolor. In the lower crypts, rows of cylindrical cryogenic bacterial DNA dewars ('Living Ink') are drawn with technical precision. 

In the center of the hall, wooden drafting desks are depicted with magnifying brass loupes, optical reading prisms, leather-bound manuscripts, and copper pneumatic message tubes running along the vaulted stone ceiling. 

Framed by fine pencil drafting construction marks, compass degree circles, vertical depth measurement scales showing depth below ground level in meters, and handwritten cursive archival notes in sepia ink. Authentic Da Vinci sketchbook aesthetic with paper foxing, watermarks, and worn parchment edges. Highly detailed, contemplative, historical engineering blueprint.
```
