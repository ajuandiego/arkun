# Eden Dome Alpha & Sector 09 (Architectural Cutaway & Cross-Section)

> **Format:** Ready-to-copy prompts optimized for Gemini / Imagen 3.  
> **Style Anchor:** Leonardo da Vinci architectural study, anatomical cutaway drawings on aged parchment, brass ruler guides, compass arcs, and selective watercolor washes.


### Prompt
```text
An architectural masterplan and cross-sectional elevation drawing in the style of Leonardo da Vinci's notebook studies, depicting Eden Dome Alpha and its surrounding Sector 09 Gray Ring on aged, fibrous parchment.

The central drawing shows a massive structural cutaway: to the left, the interior of the gleaming Eden Dome Alpha with delicate geometric geodesic truss ribbing, soaring corporate spires, lush terraced hydroponic gardens tinted in translucent green watercolor, and the central atmospheric filtration core. Separating the dome from the outside is a colossal 100-meter titanium perimeter bulkhead wall, detailed with mechanical sluice gates, sentry turrets, and exhaust vents.

To the right of the wall, the chaotic, multi-tiered slums of the 'Gray Sector' are sketched in dense, layered cross-hatching—showing stacked shipping container apartments, precarious scaffolding, steam-vent scrubber towers, and crowded bazaars. 

Drawn with graphite pencil, iron-gall sepia ink, and ruler construction guidelines. Faint compass circles indicate blast radii and air filtration ranges. The margins are filled with handwritten technical annotations, structural load diagrams, tensile stress calculation brackets, and scale rulers. Heavy parchment texture with tea stains, creased edges, and authentic Renaissance sketchbook character. No modern CAD renders, no 3D digital modeling.
```

*   **Aspect Ratio:** `16:9` (Wide landscape)
*   **Recommended Negatives:** `modern CAD blueprint, digital wireframe, neon cyberpunk colors, glossy 3D rendering`
