# Redoubt Station 14 & The Solar Farm Ruins (Tactical Field Sketch)

> **Format:** Ready-to-copy prompts optimized for Gemini / Imagen 3.  
> **Style Anchor:** Leonardo da Vinci architectural study, anatomical cutaway drawings on aged parchment, brass ruler guides, compass arcs, and selective watercolor washes.


### Prompt
```text
A battlefield reconnaissance sketch and tactical terrain study in the style of an 18th-century military engineer's field journal, drawn on rough, stained parchment with graphite pencil and brown sepia ink.

The sketch depicts 'Redoubt Station 14' nestled among the crumbling ruins of an ancient photovoltaic solar farm in the Rust Barrens. Hundreds of shattered, angled solar mirrors rise from the sand dunes like obsidian headstones, shaded with fine diagonal cross-hatching. In the center sits the reinforced concrete bunker of the redoubt, showing blast-damaged armored doors, communication antennae, and perimeter trenches.

Overlaid across the sketch are tactical field markings: circular pencil arcs drawn with a compass indicating defensive firing lines and EMP trap triggers, prevailing wind vector arrows for dust storms, elevation contour lines, and handwritten scout warnings ('Consortium patrol corridor', 'Basalt viper nests'). Soft, subtle watercolor washes of burnt ocher and rust. Weathered paper texture, frayed edges, authentic expeditionary notebook feel.
```

*   **Aspect Ratio:** `16:9` or `4:3`
*   **Recommended Negatives:** `digital minimap, modern satellite photo, clean vector art, saturated vibrant colors`
