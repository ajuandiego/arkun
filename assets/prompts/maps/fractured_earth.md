# The Fractured Earth in Year 40 AS (Orbital Globe Codex)

> **Format:** Ready-to-copy prompts optimized for Gemini / Imagen 3.  
> **Style Anchor:** Hand-drawn Renaissance manuscript on aged parchment, sepia ink linework, geometric compass/ruler guidelines, and delicate watercolor tinting.


### Prompt
```text
An authentic Renaissance da Vinci codex astronomical illustration of planet Earth in Year 40 After the Storm, depicted as an orbital celestial globe drawn on aged, weathered vellum parchment. 

The spherical planet is hand-drawn with precise sepia ink linework and graphite shading, framed by visible drafting construction lines, faint geometric compass arcs, celestial coordinate circles, and manual brass ruler markings along the margins. 

The globe illustrates the catastrophic geological results of 'The Great Cleave': Central America has vanished into a wide, dark oceanic breach connecting the Atlantic and Pacific; the Red Sea rift has expanded into an open sea-channel completely severing the African landmass; and pre-collapse coastal plains are drowned under dark waters. On the scorched northern continent, a distinct, swirling equatorial band of toxic Amber Haze is lightly tinted in translucent amber-ocher watercolor. Eden Dome Alpha glows as a delicate, solitary hexagonal jewel in pale emerald-green ink. In high geostationary orbit directly above the northern dome, the extraterrestrial biomechanical city-ship 'Spire Prime' is drawn in exquisite technical detail, connected to the mountaintops by a faint vertical sky-tether line.

Surrounding the globe are handwritten cursive technical annotations, Latin astrological and meteorological margin notes ('Orbis Fractus Post Tempestatem'), degree scale bars, and a classic Renaissance celestial compass rose. Warm cream and sepia background with antique paper texture, light foxing, and natural deckled parchment edges. Highly detailed, authentic historical sketchbook aesthetic, zero 3D digital render, zero glossy CGI.
```
