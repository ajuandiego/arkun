# The Cleaved World & The Fog of War (Cartographic Codex)

> **Format:** Ready-to-copy prompts optimized for Gemini / Imagen 3.  
> **Style Anchor:** Hand-drawn Renaissance manuscript on aged parchment, sepia ink linework, geometric compass/ruler guidelines, and delicate watercolor tinting.


### Prompt
```text
An elaborate 16th-century style Renaissance cartographic map of the fractured post-collapse Earth in Year 40 AS, drawn on a large sheet of antique, tea-stained parchment paper with visible compass lines and drafting grid marks. 

The world map features drastically altered continental landmasses showing the Great Cleave: drowned coastlines, severed continental bridges, and jagged tectonic scars. The northern refuge continent ('The Boreal Redoubt') is drawn in dense, meticulous cartographic detail with mountains, ruined rail lines, and the glowing hexagonal seal of Eden Dome Alpha and Sector 09. Across the vast oceans—labeled in ornate Renaissance calligraphy as 'Mare Stygium' (The Stygian Oceans)—the seas are depicted with violent hand-drawn wave crests, electrical lightning storm glyphs, whirlpool vortexes, and archaic Latin warnings ('Hic Sunt Tempestates Electromagneticae' / 'Nullus Redit').

In the remote southern ocean quadrant, shrouded in faint stippled cloud shading, a faint, dashed navigational corridor is sketched in crimson ink, labeled 'The Ghost Vector of Thorne', leading toward an unmapped, mountainous volcanic caldera labeled 'Terra Ignota / The Verdant Cradle'. 

The border of the map is framed by a double-ruled manual brass drafting border with latitude and longitude degree ticks. In the upper corners, detailed pencil illustrations depict hand-held drafting dividers, calipers, and an ornate nautical compass rose with intricate wind lines traversing the map. Antique parchment texture, stained vellum, edge wear, historical archival manuscript aesthetic. No modern computer graphics, no digital satellite imagery.
```

*   **Aspect Ratio:** `16:9` or `3:2` (Landscape)
*   **Recommended Negatives:** `modern GPS map, bright neon colors, modern typography, digital vector art, smooth 3D terrain, plastic textures, modern world map`
