## Purpose

The consummation. He stops if she says stop. Caelia hears what she was.

## POV

Vram, first person, present.

## When

The vault, after the compact in the basement has had days to be true. The manuscript's line that the words are only a few days old is the old clock.

## Setting

A curtained alcove. Gideon is in the lab. A vent above them.

## Story

- He cleans the burn on her shoulder.
- The compact is already in his chest, said in Kira's basement after he refused to take the rescue as payment.
- There is not an empire of glass or gold that could buy her breath from his hands.
- She tells him not to spend a speech if he means a night.
- Mean the night.
- He means the night, the morning, and the part where he stops if she says stop.
- Then do not stop yet.
- They are slow because the yard was not, and because the basement was a stop.
- Mouth on her.
- Her mouth on him.
- He governs the heat.
- She tells him, clear, to come up.
- She takes him in with her eyes open.
- The wing curves around the cot.
- He follows her over and stays.
- The socket is quiet because her palm is on his neck, and because he would stay if it screamed.
- In the vent, Caelia can see them.
- She does not preach.
- A radio in her hand carries the Forger's voice, not meant for her: bait, disposable, spend the hand if he will not take it.
- Caelia's breath breaks.
- She crawls back.
- He does not call out.
- Tsunari heard it too.
- Caelia will break, or she will come back with a knife.
- Let her choose in the hall.
- This room already chose.
