# Chapter 36 // Tsunari

> **An Ember.** One of the few who survived what the cult puts in a vein. Strong past sense, hot, and sure. The pain at the edges of the body has been burned almost away.

***

The sump water is knee-deep and the wrong kind of warm.

We are off the rail. The trolley is a dark shape behind us, Boran's line ended where he said it would. The vault is a direction, not a door. Sulfur crusts the pipe. My shoulder has closed to a tight pull. His wing drags a furrow in the water, and every drag costs him a breath he pretends is nothing.

"Ten paces," I say. "If something drops, you do not spend a leap you don't have. I will make the bend."

"I heard you."

The first body drops from a side pipe without a splash she bothers to hide. Young, hot, sure of the brand at her throat, the skin around it healed smooth. She looks at Vram as if a story has walked into her house. Then she looks at me.

"Cold Serpent," she says. "You don't get to put him out."

"Caelia," he says. A warning. She does not take it.

She hits me in the water. The strength is past sense, happy to spend itself, no flinch when her own knuckle splits on my teeth. I go under. The warm wrongness of the sump gets in my mouth. I come up with a hook in my hand and blood on my lip, mine or hers, the water makes them the same. Her skin is fever-hot against my wrist. My skin takes some of the heat and gives nothing back she can use. She laughs, bright, offended that I am still a problem.

"He falls from the sky and you crawl next to him like a leash. I was promised the right hand."

"You were promised a sentence."

I slam her into the pipe wall. The brand hits stone. She does not drop. I rake for the knee under the water, where a sure body still has to stand. She catches my wrist. The grip is hotter than the sump. I break it with the pommel of the hook, not the beak, because a beak in this water spends itself on stone. She rocks and comes back smiling.

"Come and smother him, then," she says. "I'll cut the hand off before it touches his fire."

Two more are in the tunnel behind her, the same burned-clean eyes, waiting for her to make the death neat. They have not drawn yet. She wants the story to be one woman and a knife.

Vram is still those ten paces back. The crank-hours are in his shoulder. The wing will not give him a leap. She wants me dead before he arrives. I put a bend in the tunnel between her and a straight rush, shoulder to the crust, hook low, the bad arm tucked so she has to reach across herself to take it. I bleed from the lip where her knuckle found me. I stay on my feet.

She comes again, and this time she is looking past me.

Black feathers shift in the dark at his back. She sees them. Her face opens. The hook in her hand lowers without her telling it to. The two behind her are still in the water, and the knife she promised my hand is still in her fist.
