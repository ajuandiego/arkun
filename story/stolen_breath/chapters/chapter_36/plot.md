# Chapter 36 // Tsunari

Locked: no

Part III.

## Purpose

Caelia tries to make the death neat before he arrives.

## POV

Tsunari, first person, present.

## When

After the two weeks. The walk off the rail.

## Setting

Sulfur aqueducts under Sector 09. Knee-deep water, the wrong kind of warm. Vram ten paces back. The wing drags.

## Story

- The trolley is a dark shape behind them.
- The vault is a direction, not a door.
- She tells him that if something drops, he does not spend a leap he does not have.
- She will make the bend.
- Caelia drops from a side pipe without a splash she bothers to hide.
- Young, hot, sure of the brand at her throat.
- She looks at him as if a story walked into her house, then at Tsunari.
- Cold Serpent.
- You do not get to put him out.
- He says her name.
- She does not take the warning.
- The strength is past sense.
- No flinch when her knuckle splits on Tsunari's teeth.
- Under the water, up with a hook, blood on the lip.
- The brand hits stone.
- She does not drop.
- Tsunari goes for the knee, where a sure body still has to stand.
- Two more wait behind her.
- They have not drawn.
- Caelia wants the story to be one woman and a knife.
- Black feathers shift.
- Caelia's face opens.
- The hook in her hand lowers.
- The knife she promised Tsunari's hand is still in her fist.

## Handoff

He arrives. The blood on her mouth is the fact.
