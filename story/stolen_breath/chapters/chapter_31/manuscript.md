# Chapter 31 // Vram

> **A cache.** A scavenger's store, marked and kept. Come into it bleeding and you are a bill.

***

Her blood is the thing that wakes me. Copper, close, hers, brighter than the chemical still in my shirt.

The lead dog is in the container, shoulder already open, gathering for her. I get a hand on its skull. The weight of the wing comes down on its back because I have nothing else that is heavy. No fire in the veins. No second engine. Mass, and a neck that turns too far. It drops. The sound it makes is a dog's sound, short, and then the pack outside answers it.

I do not have another of those. The torn vane drags. My hands are in the fur and the blood, and the fever is a roar behind the eyes that her proximity is only thinning, not ending.

A crawler hits the aisle with its lamps killed. The engine is a low diesel knock. A flare-slug goes up and bursts phosphor over the dogs. They scatter into the next dark. The woman on the deck has a gun, and the gun stays on my chest.

Wolf, by the shoulders. The hair goes thick there and down the upper arms, her own hair. The chest is skin. The face is a woman's face. Amber eyes, round pupils, a shine in the low light when the phosphor dies. Bare nape. No socket. The upper teeth show when she talks.

"This is my cache," she says. "The dogs have been eating it. You are bleeding on it. Give me a reason not to finish what they started."

"Kira Brandt."

"And you are a raven hot enough to pull a satellite onto my road." The gun does not wander. She looks at Tsunari's throat, at the absence of a socket, and her mouth changes by a degree. Recognition, not mercy. "The heat I can mask. I sell that. I do not haul a collar for free, and I do not haul a page I have not read."

Tsunari holds out the copy. Her hand shakes. She does not beg. "Fourteen months. The lee over the Ring fails. Evacuation is zero. You run freight through that air. Read it before you decide we are a bill."

Kira takes the page with her free hand. She reads it the way a woman reads a route, mouth moving on the numbers, the gun still honest. The last line is the one that costs her. The barrel dips, not down, aside.

"Get in," she says. "You pay for the mask with the rest of that file, and you stay where I put you. Boran owes me a door. Two fugitives and a true page clear the debt. He still decides if the bulkhead opens. I don't."

I get Tsunari up. The wing is a ruin I drag. The crawler deck is grease and rope and a heat I can already tell she means to hide, not advertise. No miracle in the veins. A scavenger, a price, and a machine that is not the government's. I can live with a bill. The dogs are already in the dark beyond the flare, learning our direction, and the crawler is the only answer I have for them.
