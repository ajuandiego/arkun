## Purpose

He spends one minute so she lives. Kira names the ampule she does not have.

## POV

Vram, first person, present. He is only up for the kill.

## When

The crash night. The loading dock, then gone again.

## Setting

Kira's cache. The aisle. The crawler with its lamps killed.

## Story

- Her blood wakes him.
- Copper, close, hers.
- He gets a hand on the lead dog's skull.
- The weight of the wing does the rest.
- No fire in the veins.
- No second engine.
- The dog drops.
- Then he is gone again, still breathing.
- Kira Brandt comes in because the dogs have been eating her cache.
- Flare-slug.
- The pack scatters.
- The gun stays on his chest until the page is read.
- Wolf by the shoulders: her own hair thick there and down the upper arms, chest skin, a woman's face, amber eyes, bare nape, no socket.
- She knows a quench ampule, a one-use legion dose that pulls a downed raven's furnace back under the number that stops the heart.
- It does not grow a vane.
- It does not turn the gain down.
- It is not the later cure.
- The cardiac shock is a different machine, already fitted, and it stays unfired.
- She does not have an ampule on the crawler.
- The mask paste is what she sells, and it is not enough.
- The bargain is the page, the paste, and the name of a locker.
- Boran's door stays shut until Vram can walk.
