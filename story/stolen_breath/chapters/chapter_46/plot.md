## Purpose

Two soldiers get a minute of quiet. Nothing leaves in a pocket. The Ring does not get a mist.

## POV

Tsunari, first person, present.

## When

The vault. The squad arrives without Cassian. They do not know he is dead. She does not tell them in a room full of triggers.

## Setting

The Great Hall of Quartz.

## Story

- Aeros-Legion 7 corners him with drawn rifles.
- Rook talks so the room cannot go holy: if they are shooting the commander, note that his optics were dusty last time and somehow the commander lived.
- Toby lowers his gun first.
- The vane is still split from the drone.
- He does not ask where his brother is.
- She does not tell him.
- She names Veda and Rook.
- Rook is Ferrin Calder.
- She is a hand.
- If they want the noise to stop for one minute, they say so.
- If they do not, they shoot.
- Veda nods.
- Palm beside the socket.
- The tremor dies.
- Rook offers his throat.
- The same minute.
- She takes her hands back.
- Gideon is still in the lab with a thing he will not call finished.
- Two soldiers get a minute.
- The Ring does not get a mist.
- Toby presses a small carved quartz hawk into her palm.
- In case she forgets she can fly.
- A bird, not a claw.
- Rook says too late, and turns his rifle toward the door the company will use.
- The hall mutinies with safeties and a choice.
- The order in their skulls is still treason.
- They make it anyway.
