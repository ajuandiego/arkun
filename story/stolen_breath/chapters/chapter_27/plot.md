## Purpose

One attempt to fly. The shoulder goes. Then the yard leaves him.

## POV

Vram, first person, present.

## When

The same night. His last conscious minutes in the yard.

## Setting

The containers of the freight yard. Kira's basement and Boran's door are under it. He will not reach either.

## Story

- He surfaces with the furnace looking for a sky.
- The shredded wing will not hold.
- He tries to lift them off the yard anyway.
- The vane spills air.
- The shoulder goes, a wet give.
- He gets her as far as the containers, his body between her and the grit, and the fever is past the number he can think through.
- Past 108.
- There is no second country.
- He does not spend Kira's name or Boran's door as a hope.
- He puts her down.
- Then he stops answering again.
- Alive.
- The rail waits out the next two weeks.
- He will not walk it in the morning.
