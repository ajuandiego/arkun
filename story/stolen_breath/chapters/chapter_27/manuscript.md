# Chapter 27 // Vram

> **The furnace.** The heat that flies the wing. Tear the wing and leave him on the ground, and the heat has nowhere to go but into him.

***

I try to lift us when the lights have gone to the lip and the containers are a maze I can use.

The wing will not hold. The torn vane spills air in a white rag of feather. The shoulder goes with a sound I feel in my teeth, a wet give, and the beat dies halfway through. We hop a container. The next beat fails. I drop us into the aisle with my body between her and the grit, the ruined wing a weight I turn so it does not land on her. The heat that should have gone into the sky stays in the chest. It climbs. Past the standing line. Past the number I can think through. A hundred and eight is a place I know, and I am past it, and the past has a color.

"Don't," she says. Her hand is on my sternum. The quiet tries to arrive and the pain rides over it. This hurt is the leash and the torn root agreeing with each other. "You fly that and you spill us on the pad where the guns are bored."

"I know."

There is no second country on the other side of the containers. The ruin is the country. The drain we climbed out of. A basement smell from a seam in the foundation, damp and old, someone else's cache. Kira's name is a rumor soldiers tell when a yard has a lock they cannot pick. Boran's door is the same kind of story. Under this floor, if the stories are true. I cannot dig a story with one wing, and I will not spend a name I am not sure I heard.

The fever crests. The aisle narrows to her face and the hand on my chest. She pushes me down against a container wall and keeps the palm there. The mesh goes softer. It does not go kind. I am still burning. The sweat on my neck freezes at the edge of the cloth and the skin under it does not.

"Listen," I say. The words are thicker than I want them. "If I stop making sense, you take the slate and you go north by the wall. You do not stay to argue with a man who is cooking."

"I don't take orders from a fever." Her palm stays. The quiet holds a small patch under it and nowhere else. "We wait until the wing remembers it is a wing. Until then you are cover. You are not a plan, and you are not a corpse I leave in an aisle."

I laugh once. It hurts the shoulder and I do it anyway, because she is right and the right has her voice on it. The yard ticks. Far off, a dog barks and then thinks better of it. Another answers, shorter. A pack deciding whether the yard is worth the walk.

"They smell the blood," I say.

"They smell both of us." She looks down the aisle, hooks already in her hands, the bad shoulder held in. "You sit. If they come, I am the one who can still move. You do not spend the wing on a dog."

I put my head back against the metal. The heat stays in me, looking for a sky it cannot have. Her hand does not leave. Somewhere under the containers a seam breathes colder air than the band.
