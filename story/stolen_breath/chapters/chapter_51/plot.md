Part IV.

## Purpose

The palms, the sister's name in the root, and the abduction. The cure is not finished. The Cradle is not reached.

## POV

Tsunari, then Vram. First person, present.

## When

Dawn after the siege. Then that night, before the next dawn.

## Setting

The scrubber roof. Then the bunker under the Vault. The salt road is west. People taken that way do not walk home.

## Story

- Dawn.
- Smoke.
- Caelia breathes because Tsunari made her breathe.
- Ugly work, not a hymn.
- Chen's people have the stills.
- The shelters will open when the freeze drops.
- The company has pulled back to count its losses.
- The cult has scattered into the pipes.
- Kira takes a sealed case from Gideon and a copy of the date.
- He tells her, in front of Tsunari, that the case is not a sky.
- She takes it anyway.
- The Ring stays under the same lee.
- Sora stays inside the glass.
- They cut their palms on a sheared girder and press them together.
- Nothing.
- Or partners.
- She kisses him at dawn like a woman who intends to keep the morning.
- The root opens on Gideon's bench because the Vault has the other half of the key.
- Fourteen months.
- The pylons south of the ring turned up, the lee fails, the dome seals.
- Under that, the line that would not open on the service door: when the sky is finished, the lower labs are a liquidation.
- Culture Lab 4.
- Sora Thorne.
- Marked.
- She puts her forehead on the girder until the want to run at the glass passes.
- Running does not open a dome.
- They sleep in the bunker because the roof is a target.
- For one hour he thinks the hour will hold.
- The vapor comes in the vents, sweet and thick.
- His knees go.
- Chains bite.
- Hands drag.
- West.
- He tries to vent.
- Nothing is left in the wing but a man's anger.
- They take him anyway.
- She wakes to a cold sheet and a chevron still smoking on her left shoulder.
- The hook is in her hand.
- He is not in the room.
- The palm aches where his blood dried.
- She looks at the door and starts walking.
