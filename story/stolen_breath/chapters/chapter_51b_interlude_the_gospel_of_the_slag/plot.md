## Purpose

The hook into Crown of Salt. The brand is the work. A crown would be a comfort.

## POV

Close third, present. Sister Maeva.

## When

The abduction night. Three hundred feet under Sector 09. He is already west.

## Setting

A basalt cavern. A boiling fissure. Not a tour of a cathedral, and not Sector 04.

## Story

- She dips a bronze brand into the fissure and sets it to the inside of her thigh.
- The mark is a shepherd's crook split down the middle.
- Smoke.
- Her breath.
- A cost she pays in person.
- The rock hums.
- She does not go looking for the source.
- The hum is the amen.
- They already have him.
- Chains.
- The salt road.
- West of the ring.
- She does not crown him here.
- She lifts the iron.
- The mark weeps.
- She tells the fissure, and the absent man, that the furnace keeps what it takes.
- Sector 09 sleeps above her and does not know the salt has already claimed a wing.
