Part IV.

## Purpose

The wing dump, once. The staff breaks. The Forger does not get the rest of her name.

## POV

Vram, then Tsunari. First person, present.

## When

The same night. He does not learn whether Malakar climbs out.

## Setting

The gantry, then the sluice where the Forger still has the staff.

## Story

- The gantry bucks.
- The heat stops asking permission.
- Black veins stand at the neck, across the chest, and beside the eyes.
- One vent.
- The wing dumps what it has been holding since the yard.
- The flail slags in Malakar's hands.
- No title.
- No formula.
- Then Vram is empty, and the veins sink back to ordinary blood.
- He does not strike a kneeling man for a doctrine.
- He strikes the hand that is still reaching for a knife.
- Malakar goes over the side into a ruptured flume.
- Whether he climbs out is not a fact Vram gets tonight.
- He did not have the dump at the Redoubt.
- Tsunari reaches the Forger while that light is still dying on the gantry.
- The hook breaks the ritual staff.
- The sound is small.
- The weapon is in two pieces.
- The Forger says Cold Serpent.
- Tsunari says Tsune, and the Forger does not get the rest of her name.
- She throws the pieces into the sluice and goes up the spine for the man who vented and did not become the Forger's god.
- Toby flashes past a drone and does not look for a brother.
- She will not put Cassian's death in the air while the roof is still choosing sides.
