Part III.

## Purpose

He comes back. This time she does not send him home as the whole order.

## POV

Ren, first person, present.

## When

The crash night, the second time he takes the south road.

## Setting

Drew's bench, then the cart, the mirrors, the dead band, the lip of the freight yard.

## Story

- The cut link is still in his ear.
- Drew has the next tang on the cloth.
- T.J. is on the set, fourteen and precise: bearing south, past the mirrors, the prowler stayed, no body on the gate log, and he is not coming up.
- Ren is not asking.
- He is telling her.
- She says Tsune told them to turn around.
- He says she told them that so they would live, and he is done spending the night living.
- The rattle clicks when he stands.
- She puts a cartridge on the bench and does not make a speech.
- He takes it.
- The lee thins.
- The mirrors are already the lie they told Mercer.
- He counts roofs, then joints.
- The band is metal and a sweetness he should not be breathing.
- At the yard the dogs have gone quiet.
- One boot on the rim.
- Tsune between the boxes, blood on the sleeve, the page in her hand.
- She puts the schedule skin in Drew's hand.
- Fourteen months.
- The lee.
- Evacuation zero.
- And she tells them the man under the containers is alive and not answering, and that she will need them.
- He still does not climb down.
- He does not see the drain or the man.
- T.J. stays in the hole.

## Handoff

She drags Vram into a container. The crew holds on the road.
