Part II.

## Purpose

He signs four thousand people off the spill so the gardens can have a wider field.

## POV

Close third, present. High Chancellor Julian Ward.

## When

A cutaway. A dinner in the dome. It does not add a day to their clock.

## Setting

A sun-room penthouse. Real beef, real strawberries, real wine. A pregnant wife. A daughter of seven.

## Story

- The beef is cut on the bias.
- The daughter explains a horse that is white except for gray ears and lives by the fountain.
- The wife wants the athletic field wider.
- The gardeners want a rose margin.
- The sheet on the table pulls the lee back a street and a half from Sector 09's downwind wards.
- Four thousand.
- Afternoons become silt.
- Cellars pool.
- He does not say the Choke at the table.
- She asks if anyone will be angry, and she means rooms that can reach this one.
- No one who is invited to dinner.
- He signs.
- Napkin.
- Kiss.
- My blood survives.
- The rest is arithmetic.
- He pours another glass.
- The wine tastes like the year on the bottle.
- He repeats the horse's name so she knows he heard it.
- Is the horse still white if she goes tomorrow.
- The ears stay gray.

## Handoff

Under the yard, he is unconscious. Her crew is coming back.
