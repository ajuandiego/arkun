## Purpose

He refuses the name. He asks Tsunari if she can walk, where Caelia can hear who he is asking.

## POV

## When

The same fight, the minute he reaches them.

## Setting

The drainage sluice. Knee-deep water.

## Story

- The two behind Caelia are still standing.
- The torn vane puts the first into the pipe.
- The heel of his hand takes the second under the brand, where the heat of them does not make a plate.
- They stay down.
- The water is a bad place to stand up proud.
- Caelia kneels.
- Palms up.
- The knife is under the water.
- She does not look for it.
- The fire knows you.
- Take my hand.
- He shoves her to the crusted wall.
- She is bleeding.
- You did that.
- He checks the lip, the teeth, the eye that is already counting, the shoulder.
- The cut is small.
- His hand stays on Tsunari's jaw.
- Caelia puts the hand out again.
- He does not take the name.
- Promised king is a collar.
- He has worn one.
- He asks Tsunari if she can walk, though she already said it, because the girl should hear who he is asking.
- Do not make Caelia a project.
- Make a direction.
- The vault.
- Through them if they stand in it.
- Caelia stays on her knees.
- She does not pick up the knife.
- He gives her their backs.
- Ahead, the pipe widens toward a light that has voices in it.
