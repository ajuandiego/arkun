# Chapter 37 // Vram

> *The fire will know you. He will take your hand.*

***

Her blood is on her mouth. The rest of the tunnel can wait.

The two behind Caelia are still standing, and standing in this water is a choice. I hit the first with the wing. The torn vane still has weight, and weight is enough to put a sure body into the pipe. The second comes at Tsunari's bad shoulder because the blood marked it. I take that one with the heel of my hand, under the brand, where the heat of them does not make a plate. They go down and stay down. The water is a bad place to stand up proud, and neither of them tries the pride a second time.

Caelia drops to her knees when she sees my face. The brand at her throat shines. Her hands rise, empty, palms up, the offer already practiced. The knife she had for Tsunari's hand is under the water. She does not look for it.

"You know me," she says. "The fire knows you. Take my hand. I was told I stand at your right."

"You were told wrong." I shove her aside, firm enough that she has to catch herself on the crusted wall. "She is bleeding. You did that. Move."

I get Tsunari's chin in my fingers. Lip split. Teeth still hers. The eye is already counting the two in the water and the girl on her knees and the dark ahead. The cut is small. My hand stays on her jaw.

"Shoulder," I say.

"Closed. It pulls. I can walk." She spits pink into the sump and does not apologize for the spit. "The two behind her waited. She wanted it neat."

Caelia makes a sound that is a sob with a snarl in it. She gets a knee under herself and puts the hand out again, closer, as if the shove had been a mistake I would correct if I saw the palm.

"She is the cold. She puts the light out. I saw it. The Forger said the hand that smothers you is hers, and I was supposed to cut it off before you had to look at her."

"I don't take the name you're offering." My hand stays on Tsunari. I keep my eyes on the cut. "Promised king is a collar. I have worn one."

Caelia's eyes burn. She will remember the shove. I will remember the blood, and which mouth it came from.

"Can you walk?" I ask Tsunari, though she already said it. I want the word while the girl can hear who I am asking.

"I was walking before you arrived." Her voice shakes. I do not soothe it. "Don't make her a project. Make us a direction."

"The vault. Through them if they stand in it."

Caelia stays on her knees as we pass. She does not pick up the knife. She watches my hand on Tsunari's back and does not get up. I give her our backs, and the tunnel, and the sound of two people walking toward a door she was not promised. Behind us the two in the water breathe and do not follow. Ahead, the pipe widens toward a light that has voices in it.
