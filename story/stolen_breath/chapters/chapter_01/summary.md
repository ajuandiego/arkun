* **POV:** Tsunari
* **Setting:** The perimeter maintenance duct of Eden Dome Alpha.
* **Job:** She unseats the Bodkin, seats the service tang, and copies the packet onto the slate. Ren is on the radio. He counts the window and tells her when the crawler is still sitting. T.J. is on the same channel from the bunker he never leaves: the silt, the dome traffic, a prowler if one paints the gate. He talks while she works. Both of them go quiet when she reads the schedule skin aloud. She does not say Sora's name on the channel. The header names the packet: the Lazarus Key. The schedule skin reads in her language. In 14 months the lee over Sector 09 collapses, barrens-air is pushed over the Ring, evacuation is zero, and the dome is sealed. The root under the header will not open.
