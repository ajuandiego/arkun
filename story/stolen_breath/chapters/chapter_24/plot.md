Part II.

## Purpose

The sky, the flak, and the hose she sees before he turns for it.

## POV

Tsunari, first person, present.

## When

The same flight. She has never been held up by a wing.

## Setting

Low over the band. The wall behind them. An observer holding past the lamps. The yard a few miles ahead.

## Story

- Roofs and a bridge drop are not this.
- The strap jumps against the split.
- She keeps the arm where the strap put it.
- Flak opens in the chemical haze.
- Grit in the teeth.
- Both arms around his neck.
- The hooks stay quiet so a beak does not find the socket.
- Road advice says do not look up.
- She looks.
- The wall is smaller.
- No hexes.
- His primaries cut a searchlight.
- Black.
- She is strapped to the body doing it.
- She does not count the wings behind them.
- A slug snaps past his knee.
- If he drops her, she will haunt the wing.
- He tells her to hold through the flare.
- Down is a problem he knows.
- Another flower.
- The light paints his throat and the scar at the jaw.
- Her mouth stays where it is.
- Under the grit, at the lip where the band becomes the ruin, a motor coughs.
- She says hose, left of the lane.
- The yard becomes boxes, a black mouth between them, and a white line coming up to meet the wing.

## Handoff

He hears her call, then he sees the hose.
