Part III.

## Purpose

They watch Malakar being made. They carry the picture, not the needle.

## POV

Vram, first person, present.

## When

The return into the temple they just fled. The same stretch of days as the market, before the vault.

## Setting

The distillation room behind the altar. A pipe in, a pipe out.

## Story

- The pipe still smells like the gate.
- She goes first, hooks in her hands.
- The kneelers are gone.
- The cup on the bench is a stain, sweet and thin.
- They do not stop.
- The narrow door has restraint leather on a nail, still damp.
- A cot.
- Straps chewed pale at the wrists, dark at the buckle.
- A tray washed and not washed enough.
- She seats the lead by feel.
- He covers the tell with his thumb.
- On the glass, Malakar a year younger, on a gantry, arms out, the brand fresh.
- The Forger where he has to look up.
- A line into the vein at the side of the neck.
- The scream is a man's, and then it is not.
- Vram has worn this heat with a socket instead of a needle.
- The flinch burns off.
- What stands up is sure.
- Say it.
- Yes, with no man left in it.
- Few live through it.
- Along the wall, more restraints.
- Two of them have never been darkened.
- He lived.
- She lived.
- They offered Vram an army, and the army was knees.
- She says suicidal, if the ones who live are this glad to spend themselves.
- Shock troops.
- She pulls the lead.
- The copy sits face-in against her ribs.
- We carry the picture.
- A bored step in the hall.
- They kill the tell.
- They leave by the pipe.
- He puts his body between her and the dark.
- The vault is next, if Gideon still keeps a door.
- Tonight the copy only has to leave the sump.
