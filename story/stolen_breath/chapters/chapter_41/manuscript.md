# Chapter 41 // Vram

> **The dose.** What they drink at the altar only tips the mind. What they put in a vein in the back room is what makes an Ember. Few live through it.

***

We go back because Chen's hours will not matter if the sump is building an army in a closet.

The pipe we fled by still smells like the gate, hot iron and the dust of a fall. Tsunari goes first. The hooks stay in her hands. My wing drags a whisper I cannot stop, so we wait out every scrape until the cistern's breath covers it. The kneelers have gone to count their god and come up short. Lamps are down to the ones that keep a watch from tripping. The basin holds the shape of a thousand knees and none of the knees.

The distillation room is behind the altar. Quiet. The cup is a stain on a bench, sweet and thin. We do not stop. The door we want is the narrow one with the restraint leather hung on a nail, still damp.

Inside, the needle. A cot. Straps chewed pale at the wrists, dark at the buckle where someone fought and then stopped fighting. A tray of steel that has been washed and not washed enough. A recorder on a shelf, optical, the housing warm from a hand that left in a hurry. Tsunari kills the room's small lamp. The slate's tell is the only blue. She seats the short lead the way she seats a jack, by feel, and the glass fills without putting a voice on the air.

"Thumb," she says.

I cover the tell. She does not thank me. On the glass, Malakar is younger by a year and already on his knees in his own belief. They have him on a gantry, not the cot. Arms out. The brand at his throat is fresh, the skin around it still angry. The Forger stands where he has to look up to love her. Her voice on the recording is the flat one. She does not shout.

A line goes into the vein at the side of his neck. He screams. The scream is a man's, and then it is not, the pain climbing into a register that empties out. Sweat sheets him. I have worn this heat with a socket instead of a needle. His hands open and close on nothing. The flinch at the edges of him, the small human wince when the strap bites, burns off while we watch. What is left is strength arriving too fast, the chest heaving, the eyes sure.

"Say it," the Forger says.

He says yes. The yes has no man left in it. The recording ends on his face, quiet, waiting for an order, the scream still wet on his mouth and no longer belonging to him.

Tsunari stops the glass. Her thumb stays on the tell. "Few live through it."

I look at the room instead of the file. The cot we are standing next to is one. Along the wall, three more sets of restraints. Two of them have never been darkened. The leather is still the color it was cut.

"He lived," I say. "She lived. They offered me an army and the army was knees."

"Suicidal," she says, "if the ones who live are this glad to spend themselves. Shock troops. Made in a closet we were dragged past on the way to a throne."

She pulls the lead. The copy sits in the slate, face in, against her ribs. "We carry the picture."

A step in the hall. Boot, boot, a cough. We kill the tell before the cough finishes. The step goes on and fades toward the basin. We leave by the pipe. She keeps one hand on the slate. I keep my body between her and the dark, and the torn wing comes with me.

The altar waits for the next mouth. The vault is next, if Gideon still keeps a door. Tonight the copy only has to leave the sump.
