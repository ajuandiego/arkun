## Purpose

Paradise, practiced until the hands bleed. He does not step.

## POV

Close third, present. Dr. Alistair Vance, 52. Not Toby's father. Not the training sergeant.

## When

The same night as Corvus's order. A cutaway. He does not meet the squad.

## Setting

A gilded-tier penthouse. White stone. Filtered air. A balcony five hundred feet up.

## Story

- Fifth wash.
- The knuckle splits anyway.
- Today he opened a chest and moved a heart from a finished man into a director.
- The gallery applauded.
- He bowed and did not look at the pan.
- Fruit grown for the bowl.
- Shoes together.
- Silver scalpels polished until the steel gives him his face.
- Synthetic wine.
- He waits.
- Nothing arrives.
- He swallows, rinses, sets the glass rim-down.
- Socks on the ledge.
- One hand on the rail, the other open.
- The weight becomes a question.
- He stays in the breath.
- He does not step.
- The blood on the knuckle beads and does not fall.
- No brother.
- No cream wings.
- No squad.
