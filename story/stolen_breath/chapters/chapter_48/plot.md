## Purpose

Caelia chooses the door. Tsunari keeps her breathing. It is not a hymn.

## POV

Tsunari, first person, present.

## When

The same siege night.

## Setting

The lower sluice threshold.

## Story

- Chen's steam hits the landing.
- The shock troops fire anyway.
- The channel says civilian loss inside the numbers is acceptable.
- Boran plants himself in the hydraulic door.
- The flail hits the plate and the plate holds.
- He tells the flyer to help low.
- His face is busy.
- Malakar comes through the steam at Tsunari.
- Caelia comes out of the steam between them.
- She does not preach.
- She puts her body on the arc.
- The iron takes her side.
- She stays up long enough to shove Tsunari back through the seal.
- Shut it.
- She heard what she was.
- Bait.
- She is not bait.
- Tsunari and Boran haul the door.
- The seal bites.
- Caelia slides down the inside, breathing, broken, alive.
- Tsunari presses the wound and tells her not to make it a hymn.
- Caelia laughs, wet, and says too late.
- Tell him she chose the cold one.
- He will hate that.
- Good.
- The door holds.
- The order outside still wants them dead or in a bag.
