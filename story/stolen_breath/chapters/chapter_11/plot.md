Part I.

## Purpose

He takes her to a post the city cannot hear, because he is not ready to hand her to a desk.

## POV

Vram, first person, present.

## When

The same night. The drive south.

## Setting

The driver's cabin of the prowler, south ring road, a few miles past the graveyard. About seven miles from the dome's center.

## Story

- Redoubt Station 14 is concrete, shortwave only.
- The city cannot hear what happens inside.
- The graveyard is behind them.
- The vault is still under the ring, and he is not walking her into it.
- He overrides the navigation beacon.
- The lie has not been spoken yet.
- What he has is a fever that climbs when she is across the cabin and drops when she is close, and an order that said catch her alive. Subject wanted for examination.
- The want is specific and he hates it.
- Bare shoulder, the scales, the mouth set against him.
- If he reaches, she will use it.
- He keeps his hands on the wheel.
- The post is a delay he is calling a landing.
- Shortwave will not save him if he tells the truth.
- He is not telling the truth yet.
- He is taking the long way so the desk does not get her while the number in his skull is still hers to move.

## Handoff

They are at the post. Drew is on the road behind them.
