## Purpose

## POV

## When

The same night as the tap. Minutes after she cut the link.

## Setting

The abandoned photovoltaic mirror fields outside the wall. Acid dust. Shattered collectors.

## Story

- The earpiece stays dead.
- She does not call Ren, Drew, or the bunker.
- She sets small charges among the broken mirrors and uses the rows the way she uses a street: low, the dark side, the rebound off a face of glass.
- Two scouts come into the field.
- She does not give them a muzzle flash to love.
- Shin, valve, a hook reversed on a feed.
- She spends the minute on the shape above her, not on tying a man down.
- The membranes take the sand.
- The legs take the drop.
- The graveyard is miles of black glass, the old attempt to save a climate before anyone arrived to sell a roof.
- She is pricing the wing she cannot see clearly yet: where it leaves the back, the hip, the distance a dive would need.
- She is building the box.
- The storm keeps the clinic's name out of her mouth.
