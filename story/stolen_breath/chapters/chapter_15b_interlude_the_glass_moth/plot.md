# The Glass Moth // Interlude

Locked: no

Part I.

## Purpose

One ordinary life the towers count as a number. The moth is the whole treasure.

## POV

Close third, present. Miri.

## When

A morning in the lee. Not a day added to the hunt.

## Setting

Copper slag heaps. A rusted corrugated shack. A thimble of morning dew.

## Story

- Miri scavenges the slag for anything the collapse forgot.
- She catches a glass-winged moth, rare, glowing, and puts it in an old fuse tube because that is the jar she has.
- At the shack her grandmother is coughing.
- They share a thimble of condensed dew.
- The moth lights the dark they live in.
- Nobody in this chapter knows the theft, the post, or the fourteen months.
- The dew and the cough are the stakes the rest of the book is already spending.

## Handoff

Back to the post. She is still his prisoner.
