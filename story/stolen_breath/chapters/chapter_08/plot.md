Part I.

## Purpose

Her hand shuts the leash off. He has no name for the quiet, and neither does she.

## POV

Vram, first person, present.

## When

The same minute. The grapple on the substation floor.

## Setting

The floor of the shattered substation.

## Story

- Her bare hand locks on the open collar and finds the siphon.
- The scream in the mesh flatlines.
- Ten years of drill stop.
- He goes to his knees in the sand with her wrists still in his hands, and the quiet is so total he thinks the fight has changed owners.
- It has not.
- She is as frightened as he is.
- She was taught the cool of her skin.
- She was not taught this.
- A sentence from a bench comes up and does not finish.
- The silence is not the Raptor body.
- She has no name for it.
- The quiet holds while the hand stays.
- He is close enough to catch the sage. The quiet under her palm is the gift. He holds the hand. The look at her mouth comes later, after he says they are the same.
- A dirty, uncollared runner in the dust just shut off a commander.
- If the noise stops, you report it. That rule is his. She does not know it.
- A quiet soldier is a broken soldier.
- She says his neck went still. She felt it stop. She is surprised, and she is still the runner.
- She spends the look and rips the palm off the socket.
- The drill comes back with the order: catch her alive.
- He does not call the quiet in. He takes her anyway.

## Handoff

The present breaks. The next chapter is nine years earlier, the night the raid came.
