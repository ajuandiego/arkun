## Purpose

## POV

## Setting

The Aerie. The ledge. The mess.

## Story

- Corvus wants a body, or a confirmation that does not wobble.
- Cassian gives her the clean version and hears himself doing it.
- Runner lost in the sand.
- No body.
- The commander is holding the south post until the weather clears.
- Nobody in the room knows the report is a lie, including him.
- Amari is on the ledge when he comes back up.
- The south wind did not read like a death.
- Rook makes the joke that keeps the mess from going quiet.
- Toby asks if Vram's neck was bad before the launch.
- Cassian says it was the usual burn.
- He files the unease under weather.
- He thinks his commander is burning through a hunt that went strange, then sitting on a post because the lattice is loud.
- He is afraid of the siphon and loyal to the wing.
- He does not learn what the hand can do.
