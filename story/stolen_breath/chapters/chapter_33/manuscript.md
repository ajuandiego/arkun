# Chapter 33 // Vram

> **Off the mesh.** The leash is also his map, his orders, and the voices of his wing. When that goes quiet, he is blind.

***

Dawn is a gray line under the yard door. She is asleep on my chest. The wing aches where the vane tore. The fever has backed down to a live coal, enough to think, not enough to fly. The mesh is quiet.

For ten years the noise was the map. Cassian's position when he was angry and flying anyway. Toby's breath on the channel when he was scared and lying about it. Orders. The sting that meant obey before the thought finished.

I open the channel the way I have opened it every morning since they put the tab in. Toby's name. Then Cassian's. The mesh gives me the inside of my own skull and nothing else. No breath. No joke from three levels down. No one to tell me the gain is still eating them.

I want them in pain and alive. I cannot see which. The wanting does not make a picture.

I do not wake her. The compact stands. The bill is the wing I cannot hear, and waking her would make her carry the bill as if she had signed it.

Kira's boot hits the stair. She looks at the two of us, at the wing trying to be a blanket, and snorts.

"Romantic. Also visible from a satellite if you keep glowing." She sets a tin on the step and a smaller tin beside it, the paste thick and gray. "The mask is a paste and a route. You wear the paste on the root and the socket. You walk when I say. The rail under this yard runs a few miles back under the ring, into the south sumps. A trolley, if Boran opens the door. The cult holds the water on the far end. The vault you want is past that water. I don't do vaults."

"The wing," I say. "Can they hear me?"

"Not from here. That's the point, and it's the problem." She nods at the paste. "Eat. Then you tell me whether she can walk, because I am not carrying a raven up a bulkhead so he can feel noble about the quiet."

Tsunari's eyes are open. She heard the last of it. She does not apologize for the silence in my head. I do not ask her to. Asking would be a way of taking the compact back.

"I can walk," she says.

"Then we walk." The paste goes on the root. It smells like clay and a lie. Her shoulder is under my hand on the stair, and the torn wing drags a whisper on the stone.
