# Chapter 49 // Vram

Locked: no
Plot approved: no
Chapter approved: no

Part IV.

## Purpose

An ordinary fight, as long as he can keep it ordinary. The dump stays shut.

## POV

Vram, first person, present.

## When

The same night. The roof.

## Setting

The crane gantry above the Vault. The wing carries him and complains.

## Story

- The roof is on fire in pieces.
- Drones, cultists, hawks against machines.
- Toby is the only Vance he can see, and Toby is still not asking the question Vram will not answer.
- Boran and Rook fight back to back.
- Hollow bird.
- Walking wall.
- The flail is not with them.
- It is with Malakar, on the crane spine.
- Vram goes up.
- He does not ask the wing for a miracle.
- One friendly slug snaps a drone off his line.
- Then the gantry is boots, iron, and Malakar's smile.
- Deliverer, said like dirt.
- Not yours.
- The flail comes around.
- A short beat puts it under the vane.
- The veins in his neck are veins.
- The heat is the old heat.
- If there is a door in him past this, it stays shut while he still has an ordinary fight.
- Below, Tsunari is at the sluice with blood on her arms that might be Caelia's.
- She is not down.
- Malakar comes again.
- Vram folds the wing at the last moment so the flail hits rail instead of bone.
- The rail screams.
- They both stay on it.
