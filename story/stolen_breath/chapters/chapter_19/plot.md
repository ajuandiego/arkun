# Chapter 19 // Tsunari

Locked: no

Part II.

## Purpose

She reads Sora's name on the audit, and the fight in the cellar ends the command upstairs.

## POV

Tsunari, first person, present.

## When

That same night. The retrieval lands.

## Setting

The mechanical room under Redoubt Station 14. A floor hatch. Pumps off to the side. An intake from the pad. A service jack in the wall beside the intake.

## Story

- He is in his own pain, not looking at her mouth.
- She watches his eyes anyway, against her own reasoning, looking for how deep it sits.
- Then the set jerks.
- He says down.
- She pulls the hooks off her hips, finds the ring in the floor, and drops.
- The room is low and she can stand.
- The pumps tick beside her, not under the hatch.
- The wire ends in a service jack.
- Tang in the recess.
- The slate lead clips to the pommel.
- The bodkin has no screen.
- The glass fills: fever, a flat place, fever.
- Analyst: Tech-Specialist S. Thorne.
- Culture Lab 4.
- Tonight, on the service door, T.J. got as far as the S and she stopped him.
- The rest of the name finished in her head.
- She wipes the session off the slate.
- The glass goes blank.
- Then she pulls the tang.
- Wings and a searchlight over the intake.
- She does not measure a span from a shadow.
- The wolf comes down the intake because the scent comes up that throat.
- Human face, human hands, a nose.
- His hands come up.
- She leaps to his shoulders before he gets a grip.
- The hook finds the strap under his ear.
- He slams her into a pump and calls the room.
- The woman rhino comes down the same intake after the shout and has the room.
- She drives her forehead into Tsunari's shoulder.
- The brow boss splits the skin only because the forehead is already on the body.
- The plate holds.
- The collarbone gap does not.
- Tsunari reaches the far wall.
- She does not see the drop.
- The blood she drew has ended the command upstairs.
