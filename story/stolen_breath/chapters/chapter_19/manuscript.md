# Chapter 19 // Tsunari

> **Plate.** The hide on a rhino's neck, shoulders, and back. A knife skips off it. The chest and the eyes are bare. A wolf follows a scent. A rhino is hide, a horn, and a short charge.

***

I am watching his eyes when I should be watching the door.

The gold in them is the quiet my hand bought. Under it he is somewhere else, on the pain, the same place the crate took him when the number climbed and my hand on his wrist was the only thing holding the climb. I know better than to stand in a man's eyes with the road still open. I do it anyway, and I go looking for how deep the pain sits.

His eyes jerk. Small. If I had been on the door I would have missed it. The quiet I was standing in is gone, and he is looking at the set as if it said his name. *You heard that before I did.*

The post's set ticks. It listens for company traffic whether he wants it or not, and the tick says someone is on the south road who did not call ahead.

"Down," he says. "Unannounced. You do not exist in this room."

I pull the hooks off my hips, find the ring in the floor, and drop through it. He lowers the lid after me, and the boots that cross it are careful. His voice goes level, the voice he uses when he is about to be a commander for whoever is about to walk in. I am the part of the room he has just erased.

The cellar is low. I can stand. The pumps tick, and the post's wire comes through the wall beside the intake. The service jack is in the plate where the wire enters. If I seat the tang I will know whether they are here for his weather or for the hour he stood six feet off me. I work the bodkin out of the braid, twist the blade off, and the tang goes into the recess. Drew would know the click. The short lead from the slate clips to the pommel-end.

Lines fill the glass. It is a telemetry audit of his post. His fever from that hour, then the flat place where my hand was on his wrist, then the fever climbing back. I know the flat place. I will not give it a name while his boots are on the lid. Under the packet, the hand that filed it.

Analyst: Tech-Specialist S. Thorne. Culture Lab 4.

The cellar goes small around that line. I know the initial. Tonight, on the service door, T.J. got as far as the S and I stopped him, and the rest of her name has been in my mouth ever since. She is reading him. *She flagged the minute.* A careful girl does that with a number that looks like a fault, and the flag has walked back down this road with soldiers.

*Off the slate, before the tang comes out.* I wipe the session. The glass goes blank. I pull the tang, seat the blade, and put the bodkin through the braid until the steel is cold against my skull. The signature stays in me, which is worse than a file.

The mouth of the intake goes dark. Wings beat over it, more than one set, and a searchlight rakes the opening and is gone. I spent the minute on her name.

"Clear the pad," a voice says, and it is not his. Company. The lid ticks once when his boots leave it. The door. Then his voice is outside, level, still trying to be the man who holds the post.

I stay off the hatch while a body comes down the intake, heavier than a man who means to talk. The air changes as his nose works it, and he has me.

I hear the breath catch when the scent finishes, and his hands come up for me. I click once, under the breath. The ceiling will not give me a second try. I kick off the pump, the membranes wipe the grit once, and the leap puts me on his shoulders before he can get a grip. The hair on the shoulder is thick under my wrist, and under it the neck is a man's. The hook's beak finds the strap under his ear and the strap opens. A socket would cook him. I need him loud enough to call the others onto the pad.

He gives me the loud on the way into the metal. He slams me into the pump housing, and the cellar rings with it.

"Here," he shouts up the intake. "She's here."

My ribs take the housing. One hand leaves my thigh and finds the braid, and he means to peel me off by the hair. I feel his face turn under my knee, a man's mouth, the strap's blood already on his lip. *Hand.* Both of mine are hooks. I let the torn strap go, bring the free hook across his wrist until the fingers open, and get two fingers to the bodkin. It leaves the hair warm. He drives me into the housing again before the point is set, and the breath goes out of me in one piece. The knife finds the soft of his throat on the way back in. He twists. The hide of his collar takes the point, and his other hand closes on my knee and starts to fold it.

A woman comes down the intake after the shout. Plate covers her neck and rides high on her back, and under it her chest is skin. She has the room the wolf had.

He drives up under me, trying to finish the fold and put me on the floor headfirst. I cannot stay on him.

I drop off his shoulders as she reaches. A hand catches the wrist with the bodkin and hauls me in close enough that her breath is on my face. The eyes above the horn are a woman's, working to find me in the bad light. She drives her forehead into my shoulder. The boss there splits the skin, and the arm goes hot. The hook she missed skips off the hide, a bad coin. I do not give it a second try. I rake the beak across the gap at her collarbone, shallow, enough to make her choose her hands.

She chooses wrong. Both hands come up to the cut, and the one on my wrist goes with them. I am past her hip and against the far wall before she gets her feet set again. The shoulder is hot under the tunic. The wolf is still in the cellar, bleeding at the strap, and his nose is still on me. She is in the room with him.

*Move.* I put the bad shoulder to the wall and move. The wing I came here with is still on the pad trying to be a commander, and the blood I just drew on a government soldier has already ended that.
