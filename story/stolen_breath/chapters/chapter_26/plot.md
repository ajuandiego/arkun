Part II.

## Purpose

Keep him from the chemical. He does not wake, and he does not get to ask.

## POV

Tsunari, first person, present.

## When

The same night. The ledge in the drain.

## Setting

The storm drain under the freight yard. The dry ledge above the wash.

## Story

- Water beads on the cloth and does nothing.
- She gets the soaked tunic off the split and his shirt off the root.
- She scrapes burned down and chemical out of the place the wing leaves the back.
- He does not make the sound.
- He does not turn.
- He does not put his mouth on her.
- The open door does not happen on this ledge.
- What she has is a man who put the wing between her and the turret and then left his own body behind.
- She talks to him anyway.
- The heart is still going.
- The fever is climbing because the wing has nowhere to put it.
- The clothes stay a ruined pile.
- Above them the grit ticks on the containers, and a light passes and does not come down the pipe.

## Handoff

He comes back once, long enough to fail a takeoff.
