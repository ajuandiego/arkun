# Chapter 35 // Caelia

Locked: no
Plot approved: no

Part III.

## Purpose

She believes. The Forger tells her what the vision meant. She walks toward them.

## POV

Caelia, first person, present. She is 21. She has been the Forger's since 14.

## When

The night before she takes the aqueduct. After a fallen raven has been long enough in the south to become a sermon.

## Setting

The Grand Cistern. Warm iron. Shin-deep water. The cup is thin rift distillate, not the vein mutagen.

## Story

- Malakar watches who drinks and who only wets the mouth.
- She drinks.
- Her hands stay her hands.
- Her heart stays the same speed until the room tips.
- A wing of fire.
- A place at his right hand with her shape in it.
- She reaches.
- Her knees hit the stone.
- Her cheeks are wet.
- The Forger crouches.
- Flat voice.
- Two fingers under the chin, hot.
- The flame that falls.
- Stand at his right hand, or go out with it.
- The cold one on the road is the hand that would smother him.
- You will not let her.
- Caelia nods with her whole chest.
- The other woman is hers to stop.
- Malakar files her as a tool.
- She sees the filing and does not spend a flinch.
- The Forger's reading is warmth, the first she has trusted since the camp.
- She stands and is already walking toward the south water.
- The ambush can wait until her feet find the pipe.
- The faith cannot.

## Handoff

They are off the rail, in the water, and she drops.
