# Chapter 35 // Caelia

> **The cup.** A thin drink of rift vapor, taken at a ceremony. It tips the room and makes a voice seem to speak to one person. It does not change the body. The Forger is the one who says what the voice meant.

***

The cistern smells like warm iron and the sweet thinness of the cup. Brands on the walls, chevrons burned into stone that used to be a sluice. People kneel in the water up to their shins because she told them the water is the altar. I am twenty-one. I have been hers since fourteen, when she took me out of a camp that had forgotten to feed the children and gave me a name with a number after it. Ember-Seven. The number felt like a room with a door.

Malakar stands at the edge of the lamp. Rail-flail across his back, quiet. He watches who drinks and who only wets their mouth and hopes the dark will count it. I drink. The cup is small. The drink is thin, vapor more than swallow. My hands stay my hands. My heart stays the same speed until the room tips.

The water light moves. The brands smear into fire and then into a wing. A voice seems to speak to me alone. Not to the row. Not to Malakar, whose eyes are still counting throats. I see a man made of falling fire, and a place at his right hand, and the place has my shape in it. I reach. The stone meets my knees. My cheeks are wet and I do not remember starting to cry.

I wake with the certainty already hard. I was there. The vision was mine.

The Forger crouches. She smells like heat and the iron of the cup. Her voice is flat. She does not need to shout in a room that has already decided to listen. Two fingers under my chin. The fingers are hot. I lean into them the way I leaned into the first meal she put in my hands.

"You saw him," she says. "The flame that falls. You stand at his right hand, or the flame goes out, and you go out with it. The cold one on the road is the hand that would smother him. You will not let her."

I nod. The nod is my whole chest. He will know me. He will take my hand. The other woman is mine to stop.

Malakar's eyes say he heard the reading and filed me under tools. I see the filing. I do not spend a flinch on it. The Forger has told me what the voice meant. Warmth, the first I have trusted since the camp.

I stand. Water runs off my knees. The row is still kneeling, waiting to be told they saw something too. I am already walking toward the south water, where the flame will be, where she will be beside him like a hand over a lamp. The ambush can wait until my feet find the pipe. The faith cannot wait. It is already in my mouth, and it tastes like the cup, thin and sweet and mine.
