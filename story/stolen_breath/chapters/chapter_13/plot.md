## Purpose

She obeys the order to go home, lies to Mercer, and keeps the clinic dark.

## POV

Drew, first person, present.

## When

The same night. The south road, then the turn back.

## Setting

The lee thinning, the mirrors, a watchpost she can see and cannot enter. Then the road home.

## Story

- T.J. is in her ear the whole way.
- He is fourteen.
- He has the bearing and the silt, and he will not come up, and she stops asking.
- Ren is beside her because he would not stay in the hole.
- She calls from the last mirror.
- She hears the order to go home, a second breath, and no name.
- The set dies.
- She turns the cart around because Tsune asked.
- She hates the mile she is walking back.
- She does not tell Mercer where the road ended.
- She tells him they lost the signal at the mirrors.
- After he leaves the room, the same shortwave gives her what T.J. kept: the beacon died, the prowler stayed past the mirrors, no body on the gate log, and the copy he saw is still on his screen.
- He does not read it on the air.
- He does not come up.
- She goes back to the tangs on the bench.
- She knows the bare nape.
- She does not say it on an open relay.
