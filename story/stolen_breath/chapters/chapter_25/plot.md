## Purpose

He takes the hit for her, flies the drain, and stops answering. The heart keeps going. The cardiac shock does not fire.

## POV

Vram, first person, present.

## When

The same night. The end of the flight.

## Setting

The dead band, then the storm drain under the freight yard. About ten miles south of the dome's center.

## Story

- She has already said hose.
- He hears the motor under her voice, then sees the white line.
- He rolls her under the wing.
- The burst finds the left vane anyway.
- Chemical soaks both of them.
- The pain is a nail through the root.
- He still aims the black mouth between the containers, because the pad is where a gun finishes a fallen wing.
- They hit water that is not water.
- He rips the strap, hauls her to the dry ledge, and the wing follows.
- He is breathing.
- Then he stops answering.
- The heart keeps going.
- The one cardiac shock fitted to a command raven does not fire.
- That shock is spent once, in a later book.
- This crash does not spend it.
- One ruin overhead.
- A basement smell from a seam.
- Cassian calls his name once.
- He does not answer.
