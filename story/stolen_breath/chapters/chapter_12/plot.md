Part I.

## Purpose

She learns the room, cuts her own people off the set, and sees what the wing actually is.

## POV

Tsunari, first person, present.

## When

The same night. Inside the post.

## Setting

Redoubt Station 14. Shortwave only. The dead band is the next mile south.

## Story

- She scans exits, the set, and anything that can become a lever.
- No line to the city.
- Anyone with a box nearby can hear the shortwave.
- T.J. stayed down and called the bearing.
- The prowler turned south.
- Drew and Ren followed.
- Drew's voice comes up, Ren behind her.
- Tsunari uses the field voice.
- Face the gate.
- If she hears the set again tonight she will assume they want a patrol to learn their names.
- She says nothing about the man in the room.
- She pulls the relay.
- He hears the name Drew.
- He does not get the clinic, and he does not get the bunker.
- He treats the wing.
- The cloth and the ice-salve come out of the harness rig.
- The salve is for reddened skin.
- It does not stop the drill.
- She sees flesh swollen on both sides of the socket, and the quills growing out of living bone.
- She asks who comes if the weather lie fails.
- He gives her Cassian, and no one else.
- The body is not the monster she priced in the sand.
- It is a body eating itself for a regime.
- She still does not thank him, and she still counts the door.

Drew hears the order and turns the cart around.
