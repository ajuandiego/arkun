# Chapter 12 // Tsunari

Locked: yes
Plot approved: yes
Chapter approved: yes

Part I.

## Purpose

She learns the room, cuts her own people off the set, and learns he has no plan past tonight.

## POV

Tsunari, first person, present.

## When

The same night. Inside the post.

## Setting

Redoubt Station 14. Shortwave only. The dead band is the next mile south.

## Story

- The ceiling is low. The wing stays folded.
- An iron stove in the corner, cold, pipe through the roof, poker beside it. She asks to light it. He says no. The pipe would smoke, and anyone on the road would see it.
- The shortwave is on a crate in the open floor. A cot is against the far wall. A rack for the harness is beside the door. The set's lead runs to the wall and down.
- A ring in the floor, between the crate and the stove, opens on the pumps. A duct runs from that room out to the pad. She asks. He tells her.
- She checks the ways out. The bar is across the door. The slit will not take a shoulder. The roof hatch is bolted from inside, and the bolts will take time.
- She asks what the plan is now that they are here. He was supposed to take her to the gate. He did not. If he calls it in, they open both of them, so he does not call. They have until the man who comes through once a day, if the room was empty. He does not know what they do in the morning. He needs time to think. He is not taking her to a table. If they still have no plan by morning, she tries the roof bolts, then the ring.
- No line to the Dome. Anyone with a set nearby can hear the shortwave.
- He took the cuffs off so she could walk. The thin broke when she stood.
- Inside, she keeps the picture of the leash off. He shakes a few steps away. The hand stays off until he asks.
- T.J. stayed down and called the bearing.
- The prowler turned south.
- Drew and Ren followed.
- Drew's voice comes up, Ren behind her.
- Tsunari uses the field voice.
- Face the gate.
- If she hears the set again tonight she will assume they want a patrol to learn their names.
- She says nothing about the man in the room.
- She pulls the relay.
- He hears Tsune, Ren, and T.J. He does not hear Drew. She does not say Drew on the set.
- He says Tsune. She tells him that if he repeats the names where a patrol can hear them, she will touch him to kill, not to help.
- He does not learn a clinic or a hole.

## Handoff

Drew hears the order and turns the cart around.
