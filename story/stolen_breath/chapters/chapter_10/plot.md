Part I.

## Purpose

She wakes alive, and she learns that her hand is the only thing keeping his fever from climbing.

## POV

Tsunari, first person, present.

## When

The same night as the grapple, minutes after. The memory was the cutaway.

## Setting

Inside an armored prowler on the wasteland perimeter.

## Story

- She wakes in magnetic cuffs, not opened on a table.
- He sits across from her.
- The harness is hot.
- The eyes are feverish.
- While her hand was on him the quiet held.
- She pulled it away to be cuffed, and the tremor came back.
- She watches his breathing the way she watches a number that will own a plan.
- She has no name for what her palm did.
- She starts using the need anyway.
- A soldier who can report her face, the theft, and the quiet is safer kept in reach.
- The file they stole is still on her, or it was.
- The prowler is going somewhere the gate will call a victory.
- She is measuring the cabin: his hands, the door, how long the quiet lasts if she gives him the wrist and how fast the drill returns if she takes it back.

## Handoff

He turns the prowler off the beacon. The post is next.
