## Story

- Six feet away from her, the fever spikes and the lattice shakes.

- He measures it and hates the measurement.
- Corvus wants the captive dead, or a body she can read.
- The order is execution.
- He says weather.
- The prisoner died in the sand.
- He is holding the south post until it clears.
- It is the first lie of his service.
- She is in the room, alive, and the quiet is the reason he will not put her on the table.
- He does not explain the hand.
- He does not give Corvus a mechanism.
- The packet can go when the beam is clean.
- Tonight the beam is how he keeps the wing from being told to come get a corpse he cannot show.
- She hears the lie.
- She does not thank him.
- A lie is a leash with her hand on it, and she knows it.
