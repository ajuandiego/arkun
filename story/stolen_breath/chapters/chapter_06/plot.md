# Chapter 6 // Vram

Locked: yes
Plot approved: yes
Chapter approved: yes

Part I.

## Purpose

He hunts a runner who prices him back, and he wants the rest of her before he drops.

## POV

Vram, first person, present.

## When

The same night, over the mirror field.

## Setting

The perimeter of the solar mirror banks.

## Story

- The order is catch the runner alive. Subject wanted for examination.
- The file comes with her.
- He could break that order in one stoop. He does not take it yet.
- From above she stays cool, the same temperature as the glass.
- The push off the bowl is a leg, higher than the suit. Not human. Not Avian. Lycan would keep the heat, Ceraton the size, Galvan the field. She kept none of them. *Unless.* He asks if it is possible. *She might be a Raptor.*
- In the shack the collar comes off the nape. Skin, no socket. The leg already said Raptor. He has no second to spend on the throat.
- From the circle, the first suit's lamp dies. The runner is on him and gone. Vram does not get the hands. The runner leaves the man breathing.
- The second fires. A bowl breaks. He loses the runner in the glass. When the dust moves, that one is down and the runner is already leaving.
- The runner spends the minute on the wing.
- He wants the slate, and he wants the next move before he takes it.
- The berm says she, and small. He had been waiting on a second body in the row. There isn't one.
- She is not admiring the bird.
- She is pricing the reach.
- He shortens the pass to see if she believes the tell.
- She moves while he is committed to a line he already chose.
- The lane he marked is empty.
- Two rows deeper.
- She used his own pass as cover.
- He hates how well the work fits, and he stays up anyway.
- Then he tucks and dives.
