## Purpose

He hunts a runner who prices him back, and he wants the rest of her before he drops.

## POV

Vram, first person, present.

## When

The same night, over the mirror field.

## Setting

The perimeter of the solar mirror banks.

## Story

- The order is recover the runner.
- The file is worth more than the body.
- Under it, hotter, is the other word: open the intruder.
- He does not take the stoop yet.
- From above she stays cool, the same temperature as the glass, no socket at the nape.
- The leash offers a tag and eats it.
- The first suit walks a trench he has decided is empty.
- A coin-sized charge on a wire folds him.
- She tells him to stay down and is gone.
- The second stays on the berm and fires.
- She kills the feed on the net gun and puts a knee through the joint.
- She does not stay to tie him.
- She spends the minute on the wing.
- She is not admiring the bird.
- She is pricing the reach.
- He shortens the oval to see if she believes the tell.
- She moves while he is committed to a line he already chose.
- The lane he marked is empty.
- Two rows deeper.
- She used his own pass as cover.
- He hates how well the work fits, and he stays in the oval anyway.
- Then he tucks and dives.
