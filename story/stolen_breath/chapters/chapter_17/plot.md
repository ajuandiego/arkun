Part II.

## Purpose

He gives her AE-701 and two human facts about the roost. He does not cross the step.

## POV

Vram, first person, present.

## When

The same night, twilight at the post.

## Setting

The watchpost balcony. Bruised sky, molten brass. The low wall.

## Story

- The number slips out.
- Subject AE-701.
- Taken from a Ring orphan ward at seven, the name scraped, the draft in its place.
- She does not use the number again.
- She asks what a day on the roost is when nobody is hunting.
- He gives her two things and will not say he likes them: Rook's ration toasted on his shoulder, and Toby's ice on the socket.
- She tells him the rolls burned a sister.
- She does not give the name.
- She does not give the lab list.
- He aches to cross the step and does not.
- She would read it as a grab, and she would be half right.
- The quiet between them is the threshold, not a kiss.
- The retrieval is still coming.
- He has only bought the weather.
