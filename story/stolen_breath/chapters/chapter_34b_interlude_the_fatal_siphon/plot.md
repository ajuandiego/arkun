Part III.

## Purpose

The gain stays at the top. It kills him. The squad is not in the room.

## POV

Close third, present. Lieutenant Cassian, 28.

## When

Day fourteen after Vram left the pad. During the basement weeks. The pair do not know.

## Setting

A roost he no longer reports to. The mess three levels down, heard and not visited.

## Story

- Fourteen days.
- The noise never came down.
- The socket is a coal.
- The tab bites.
- His hands stay flat because flat does not shake.
- The wings open once: dark slate, a pale grey along the trailing edge, not black, no sheen.
- A searchlight takes them and lets them go.
- They fold.
- The Lycan share in him is the nose and the hearing.
- He smells his own burned skin and hears Rook telling a joke that has no one left to land on.
- No fur.
- No venom.
- There is the leash, and it is enough.
- He deserted at dawn, away from the channel, and the desertion did not turn the gain down.
- He thinks of Toby, cream and rust, and does not call.
- A call would put the boy in the log beside a dead second.
- He thinks of the weather report he repeated.
- The burn goes white.
- The wings shiver and go still.
- The roost sends no squad.
- Toby does not learn.
- Forehead to the stone.
- Then the noise, and the man, stop.
- Vram is still under Kira's floor, or just now able to walk.
- Neither he nor Tsunari hears this.

Caelia's cup. The pair are about to enter the water he died during.
