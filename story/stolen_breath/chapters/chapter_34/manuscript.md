# Chapter 34 // Vram

> **The rail under the yard.** A few miles of freight line, back under the ring, into the south sumps. The vault is past that water. The cult holds the water.

***

Boran fills the door before he opens it.

He is bald, short in the neck, wide as the frame. Grey hide, cracked, covers the face, the neck, the shoulders, both arms. An old crack runs through the plate on his left cheek. A heavy horn sits on the brow, centered, close to the bone. The chest under the plate is bare skin, weathered brown. No socket. Small amber eyes under the horn. He looks at the torn vane, at Tsunari standing on her own feet, and he laughs once, low.

"Fancy fallen flyer," he says. The voice is a baritone that stays in the stone. "Clipped. And a girl who hasn't run. Kira says you clear a debt."

"The page is real," Kira says. She does not step into his shadow. "The mask is paid. The door is yours."

He grunts. My shoulder is still between Tsunari and the bulkhead. "Fine. The trolley is hand-cranked. The line runs a few miles under the ring and spits you in the south sumps. After that the water belongs to the hymn-singers. I don't walk you into a sermon."

"The vault," Tsunari says.

"Past the water. Gideon's problem. Not mine." He unlocks the brake with a key on a cord. The rail smell is old oil and cold stone. A demolition chisel rides his belt and knocks the frame once when he turns. "You crank. You don't preach on my line. You don't bleed on the seat if you can help it. The seat has carried worse, and I still prefer it clean."

I take the crank. The wing complains in the shoulder, a wet pull. She stands at the lamp and watches the dark ahead, already counting the places a body could leave the car. Kira lifts two fingers from the stair, debt spent, gun still her own, and is gone.

The trolley moves. The horn of the lamp swings. A few miles of freight line, the ring somewhere over our heads, then sump water and the people who hold it. A room past that water might tell us what her hand is. I crank. Boran watches the hide of the tunnel the way a man watches ground he intends to keep, one palm on the stone when the car sways, as if the rock had asked him to stay.
