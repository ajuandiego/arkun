Part I.

## Purpose

Put the squad on the ledge, the leash in his neck, and the only wingspan the book measures, then dive.

## POV

Vram, first person, present.

## When

The same night, the minute the siren hits.

## Setting

The launch ledge of the High Aerie, on the center mast, three thousand feet above Sector 09. No rail.

## Story

- The socket is a drill in the bone.
- An observer holds above the mast, nothing under the hull.
- There is no fuel left for a machine that has to come home, so they grew the wing.
- Rook tells him to hold still because he is toasting the flatbread.
- Cassian wants to wait on the north wind.
- Toby Vance, cream and rust, Cassian's brother, trades an ice patch onto the socket and the drill drops from a scream to a wire.
- Amari says the south face is dirty and will dump him if he takes it lazy.
- The siren hits.
- Breach, south wall, a jack in a service door.
- Catch alive.
- Subject wanted for examination.
- Cassian holds the roost until the lane is called.
- Rook and Toby take the bridge lights and do not drop for a ghost.
- The playback is a heat smear under the south bridge, already moving.
- Amari confirms the south face is still dirty and the prowler has not moved.
- He opens the wings.
- Fourteen feet.
- Black vanes.
- This is the one time the book measures the span, because it is his body and he knows it.
- A hard beat puts a blue-violet sheen on the shafts, there and gone.
- The first throw, a sergeant with no name he was allowed to keep, sits under the drop.
- The air kept him then.
- He dives.

## Handoff

He is in the air over the gate. She is already running.
