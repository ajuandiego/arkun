Part I.

## Purpose

He gives her the culture and a plain warning: skin, eyes, legs, and a body that will run colder. She finds out what that means as she grows, out of the reader's sight. He hides the rest, and leaves so the strike team follows him. The reader does not learn what the rest is.

## POV

Jeffrey Thorne, first person, present. Tsunari is seventeen. Sora is ten.

## When

Nine years before the present. The night the raid is already in the street.

## Setting

The lab under the workshop. Five dishes on the bench. Sora asleep behind the inner door.

## Story

- She is coughing the haze into her sleeve.
- He counts five dishes.
- He names one.
- A dish can be taken.
- A page can be read.
- The illness will not wait for a willing stranger.
- He can call the culture medicine.
- He also knows he is using his child as the only place the rest of the finding can live.
- He does the work and does not stop.
- What he hides has no name on this page.
- Her legs feel wrong.
- He tells her the skin, the eyes, and the legs will change, and that she will run colder.
- She will find out as she grows. That finding is not on the page.
- No one else learns it.
- He does not name the body.
- The bench sentence, until she can say it cold.
- Mercer will finish the education.
- Mercer will not get the notes.
- Sora's door stays shut.
- He sends Tsunari out the alley stair toward Mercer and will not let her open that door.
- Then he lights the crawler and makes the engine rude, so the strike team follows the noise away from both children.
- He does not burn the room while a child is in it.
- The chapter ends on that choice.
- It does not show Sora taken, and it does not show the far shore.

## Handoff

The present resumes in the prowler. She is cuffed. He is across from her.
