# Chapter 23 // Vram

> *You do not fire on your own wing.*

***

The dead band is a few miles of bad ground and a rim of pale lakebed. Chemical sheen. The air tastes like a mouthful of coin. I have her locked to my chest and the wing is doing two jobs. Fly. Do not crush her.

Her thighs stay locked. The split on her shoulder is against my plate, and the heat there is the only reason the arm has not quit. Her palm is trapped between us, flat on the socket, and the leash tries a stitch and does not get the climb. I use the quiet. The yard is still ahead of the lakebed.

Cassian is on my left. Hawk. The man I told to hold a roost until the road was clear. His hands stay open on the grips. He was told a weather report and a corpse. He is looking at a living woman in my harness, and the order in his skull is losing by a second at a time.

"Commander," he says on the channel. "Land and I can still call this a return."

"Land and the log still says weather. I am not giving them the ground."

Rook comes in on the right, fast, talking because the channel is a log and he knows who reads logs. The slug he sends goes wide into the haze, a bright wrong line that dies over empty band.

"Targeting optics full of dust!" he shouts.

The next shot is wide too. He is a hawk. He does not miss twice unless he means to. I see his mouth move on a curse he does not give the channel. For the log he is incompetent. For me he is still mine, and the gift will cook him if Corvus decides a wide shot is a choice.

Toby dives from above. Cream and rust, the small wing, younger than the order he is breaking. A flak drone has locked on the root of my wing, the ugly buzz of a thing that does not hesitate. He tucks into the slipstream and puts his body in the way. The burst chews his vane instead of my bone. Feathers come apart in the haze. He makes a sound, soft, the apology he uses when he has already done the thing.

"Kestrel," I say.

"I'm here." His voice is thin with the pain and steady under it. "Don't turn for me. I can fly ugly."

"You do not fire on your own wing," I tell the channel. I am the one who left. The sentence is mine, and I have already broken it by being the target.

Amari is higher, a pale flicker, wheat along the vane when a lamp catches her. She does not shoot. She told me the south face was dirty and she is done giving me a clean lane I will not take. Cassian still has not fired. The leash on all of them will be climbing, because a hesitation is a kind of blood and Corvus does not like it unpaid. I feel mine try again. Her palm presses, and the climb stops short of my eyes.

"Yard," she says against my throat. "Containers. A lane between them if you can still see lanes."

"Then we get there before a gun that means it."

Rook laughs once, broken, the joke with the bottom out of it. "Fly, Commander. I'll keep missing until the log gets bored."

I beat. The band streams under us, sheen and then the pale rim, and the containers stop being a rumor. Toby stays in my shadow with the torn vane spilling air. Cassian follows without firing. Her arms stay around my neck. I do not ask her to look back.
