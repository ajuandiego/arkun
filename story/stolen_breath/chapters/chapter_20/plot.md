## Purpose

He deserts. Their blood is the crime. Her hand on his wrist is the second one.

## POV

Vram, first person, present.

## When

The same minutes. Pad, then the hatch.

## Setting

The landing pad of Redoubt Station 14, then the lower mechanical room. An observer keeps its height. A prowler lands.

## Story

- One wolf and two rhinos, Directorate kit, sockets fitted, no black veins.
- The wolf's nose says there is a second body under the floor, fear and copper and a woman.
- The woman's eyes are on the intake.
- The man's shoulder is already toward the door.
- The leash climbs because the wrist is empty, and because if he gives them the hatch they open her on the pump-room floor.
- The wolf goes down the intake.
- That is where the scent is.
- The man starts for the door.
- Vram leaves the lip and puts him into the wall short of the latch, shoulder into the chest before the brow can land.
- The man stays down.
- He never reaches the cellar.
- The woman takes the mouth Vram left.
- He hears the cellar.
- He opens the door.
- The man does not get up.
- He drops through the hatch.
- The wolf is first, bleeding at the strap, and a grab from a knee will not move the wing.
- The woman is in the room, plate scored, blood at the collarbone, and she comes in on a short charge.
- He is off the floor before her brow gets there.
- The heat of the grip and the vane go into the unplated chest.
- She goes down.
- Tsunari is still at the far wall.
- Hooks low.
- Blood on the shoulder.
- She does not thank him.
- Her hand on his wrist stops the next climb.
- Wolf and woman on the concrete.
- The man on the pad, short of the door.
- Deserted the same hour.
- The gain can go up on the wing he left.
