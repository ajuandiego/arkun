# Chapter 38 // Vram

Locked: no
Plot approved: no
Chapter approved: no

Part III.

## Purpose

He refuses the army in front of the people it was promised to.

## POV

Vram, first person, present.

## When

The same day as the ambush. The cistern.

## Setting

The Grand Cistern. Water, iron catwalks, thousands kneeling. The Forger where a voice carries without rising.

## Story

- Two ahead, one behind, none of them the girl he left on her knees.
- Tsunari walks with the bad shoulder toward him, counting the rail, the winch, and the dark mouth at the left.
- She does not kneel when the rows do.
- A man in the second row has his eyes shut and his mouth moving on a name that is not Vram's.
- A woman beside him has a child's wrap folded in her lap, empty.
- Caelia is in the front row, shining and wrecked, watching his hands.
- Malakar's flail gives one pneumatic hiss, then goes quiet.
- The Forger's chest is bare above the brands once.
- He looks at her eyes.
- Flat.
- Deliverer.
- An army.
- Caelia as the right hand.
- The cold one is the price.
- Cut her here, and the flame stays lit.
- The rows answer the word army like relief.
- Tsunari is looking at the sluice winch.
- He spits it so the cistern can keep it: she is not my bride, and I am no one's god.
- The kneelers make a sound like a wound.
- The man closes his hand on the empty wrap.
- Then you are slag.
- Sweep.
- Caelia's joy is gone.
- Malakar roars.
- Tsunari is already moving for the sluice.

## Handoff

The flail needs room. She takes the room away.
