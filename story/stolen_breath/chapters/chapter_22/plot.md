Part II.

## Purpose

She locks onto a man she fought tonight and does not let go.

## POV

Tsunari, first person, present.

## When

The same night. Minutes after the cellar.

## Setting

The pad, the south lip, the air over the dead band. Containers are still a rumor.

## Story

- The wolf is on the concrete.
- The woman is down by the pump.
- The man is on the pad, short of the door, alive.
- A mouth there will tell the next wing which way they stepped.
- She says she can run the band.
- He says the first lamp finds her.
- He pulls the spare passenger harness and routes the strap under the split shoulder, not across it.
- Thighs lock or she falls out of the story.
- She shuts her eyes for the glare, not for the order.
- A voice on his old channel starts the cadence.
- She does not take the words.
- He cuts it with his jaw.
- Some of them will miss on purpose.
- Do not count on the kindness.
- He steps off.
- The stomach goes, the boots find nothing, and the wing takes the air.
- She is locked to a soldier she fought in the shack tonight.
- A flare opens.
- Lamps, and a line of wings.
- She stops measuring.
- Her weight is in his harness.
- She does not let go.

## Handoff

His squad is in the air behind them. The chase is the next chapter.
