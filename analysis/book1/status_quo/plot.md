# Plot

What has closed, and what the last pages leave standing. The chapter promise is `canon/plot/book1/`. Later books are named here only as doors the reader has not walked through.

## Lived

The reader has watched the hunt become a choice.

* Tsunari steals the Lazarus Key. The surface schedule is already ugly: in fourteen months the lee over Sector 09 fails, barrens-air comes over the Ring, and the dome seals.
* One touch quiets Vram's lattice. He hides her, lies to Corvus, and kills an Inquisitor rather than let her burn.
* The barrens and the sump strip the rank off him. The Forger offers a throne and a bride. He refuses both.
* The Vault gives each of them a past the company did not write. His genome is ancient. Her blood carries five frequencies. A local stabilizer, made there, quiets Veda and Ferrin.
* Sector 09's battle turns. Corvus's purge retreats. The cult scatters. Chen holds the stills. Caelia lives. Kira leaves with the drives.
* They choose each other in the open, after the sex in the alcove and again as a blood compact at dawn.
* Before the next dawn the zealots gas the bunker, brand Tsunari, and take Vram.

## Named

The reader can say these aloud. The book has not finished them.

* The deep layer of the Key, decrypted on the scrubber roof: the schedule is alien terraforming, and the lower staff of Eden Dome Alpha, including Sora, are marked once it finishes.
* The Ghost Beacon. Jeffrey's shorthand, from inside the ocean. A survival implied, not a reunion.
* The Gospel. Pylons waking under the continent. A rune for harvested cattle. A road to the Cathedral of Salt.

## Open

These are the question marks the last page is built to leave.

* Where Vram is by the time Tsunari can stand, and whether the man in the chains is still choosing.
* Whether the Forger still commands, or only Maeva's rite does.
* Whether Malakar crawled out of the flumes.
* Whether the local stabilizer is a cure or a proof. Kira has drives. The book does not show a sky full of quiet soldiers. An aerosol that frees a cohort without Tsunari's hand is a later book.
* Whether Jeffrey is a man or a recording. The Cradle is not a place this book arrives at.
* How a ring that just survived a purge lives the next fourteen months.
* The reader knows Cassian is dead. The squad does not. That gap has not been paid.

## What this book is finished with

The theft, the hunt, the defection, the cult's first claim, the Vault's proof, the Sector 09 turn, and the compact. The abduction is the hook, not a rescue.
