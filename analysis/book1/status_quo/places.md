# Places

Where the reader has stood by the end of *Stolen Breath*. The sensory home is `canon/places/locations_and_sensory.md`. Counts and the map are in `canon/world/geography.md`. This file is only the depth.

## Lived

The reader can walk these without a lecture.

* **Sector 09, the Gray Ring.** Copper on the tongue, the silt hour, water at dawn, the Night Freeze, a guild cartridge as a night's sleep and not a tomorrow. The Glass Moth puts a child in the thin lee downwind. The siege is fought in these streets, sluices, and roofs.
* **The bulkhead.** A wall you do not cross. The climate field spills past it. That spill is the lee. The reader has the height as a fact and the life under it as a feeling.
* **The High Aerie and the drop.** Vram launches from the crown. Flight is a body, not a machine. The reader does not get a tour of the roost.
* **Redoubt Station 14.** The bunker, the vents, the lie to Corvus, the Inquisitor in the shaft, the harness jump off the parapet.
* **The Rust Barrens.** Red dust, a Night Freeze, a crash, a feral pack, a crawler. The open dune is a crossing. Low ground after dark is a place you do not sleep.
* **The sump under Sector 09.** Flood, tithe, the Forger's hall, Malakar's flail, Chen's steam, Boran's door. The Gospel drops the reader three hundred feet lower, into Maeva's basalt cavern, and lets them hear the rock hum.
* **The Glass Vault.** Eighty feet down. Quartz, prisms, the Scythian plates, the spectrometer, the alcove, the hall where the squad breaks. By dawn the scrubber roof is a place where two people can sit and still be hunted.
* **A dome interior, at a distance.** Sora's log, the Sterile Room, the Quota Calculation. Jasmine, marble, a man who signs away four thousand neighbors and kisses his daughter. The reader has been in the cage. They have not been free to walk it.

## Named

The reader can point at these. They have not lived in them.

* **Eden Dome Alpha as a machine.** Five miles, the companies, Bio-Lab 4, the staff list. Sora is inside it. The purge comes out of it. The streets of the interior stay a rumor with good air.
* **The Stygian Ocean.** Boiling, ionized, impassable for forty years. A disc came out of it. No hull on the page has crossed it.
* **The Salt Flats and the Cathedral of Salt.** The last page puts Vram on that road. The reader has the direction and the name. They have not seen the door.
* **The Torrid Kiln.** A destination the Gospel aims at. No room, no smell, no rite beyond Maeva's cavern.

## Open

* What the Cathedral will look like, and whether it is a temple, a prison, or both.
* Whether any road out of Sector 09 is still open after the purge.
* The far side of the sea. The disc implies a crossing. It does not give a shore, a corridor, or a name the reader can use.
* Spire Prime, the other Spires, and any room where Xaevis stands. Barges are a fear in the sky. The citadel is not a set.
* How many other rings exist, and whether Kira's crawler can reach one before the fourteen months run out.
