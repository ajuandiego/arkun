Where the people on the page know less than the reader, the line says so.

Three depths, used the same way in every file:

* **Lived.** The reader has been inside it. A cost, a smell, a wound, or a choice.
* **Named.** The reader can repeat the fact. They have not watched the limit, or they have watched it once.
* **Open.** The question mark. The book has pointed, and it has not answered.
