# Vaelen

What the reader understands about the occupiers by the end of *Stolen Breath*. The working account is `canon/world/factions/vaelen.md`. This file does not import that account. It marks how much of it the book has spent.

## Lived

* **They are here, and they are above the law the reader knows.** Barges in the high air. Inquisitors who walk onto a human redoubt and demand a man's telemetry. One of them dies on Vram's talons, and that death is treason, not a skirmish.
* **The leash is theirs in practice.** The noise in a soldier's skull is called the voice of his country. Silencing it is the crime. Cassian dies of it. Vram's career ends when he chooses the silence.
* **The schedule is a weapon.** Chapter 1 steals a date. Chapter 44 decrypts it. Within fourteen months they collapse the lee, push barrens-air over the Ring, and seal the domes. People outside a city of this size do not live on barrens rules. Sora's lab is on the list of what gets cleaned up after.
* **Something under the ground answers them.** The Gospel puts a hum in the bedrock, fourteen hertz, pylons waking, while a priestess brands herself with a rune for harvested cattle. The reader feels the occupation move from a decree to a machine.

## Named

* **Terraforming.** The decrypted root calls the program alien, and says the domes are being kept for a colonial migration. The reader can say the sky is being changed on purpose. They cannot yet say what the new air is for, in the lung of the creature who ordered it.
* **An old visit.** Scythian plates, a Persepolis epigraph, five beasts in a first garden. Gideon uses them to unhook Vram from the vat. The reader has antiquity as a wound in the company's story. They do not have the two visits, the abandoned trials, or the reason a trait stays on this world and nowhere else.
* **Inquisitors as bodies.** Tall, chitinous, a bio-lance, an ocular cluster. Enough to fear. Not an anatomy lesson, and not a politics.
* **The lattice as pain and obedience.** Diagnostic sting, a gain that can be raised, a soldier who still flies his own body. The master kill, one hand in orbit, is not fired and not explained.

## Open

These are the question marks the Vaelen file in the bible is already answering, and this book is not.

* Why they want the planet, if not as a conquest the reader already understands. The page gives a schedule and a cruelty. It does not give the bench, the trait that stays, or the self they are trying to graft.
* What they breathe, and why the storm and the pylons are a kindness to them and a bill for everyone else. The reader has sulfur, cough, and a collapsing lee. The lung that needs that blend is still backstage.
* Who Xaevis is, and who can dissent. Lyraen is not a voice yet. A schism has not been offered.
* Whether a Vaelen can wear a human life. No violet blood, no clean filter, no mentor whose cartridges never stain. Mercer is not a reveal in this book.
* Whether the kill in the socket can still be fired after a Vault dose.
* What "colonial migration" means in bodies. Ships, a spawning, a replacement of the baseline. The words are on the decryption. The picture is not.
* The rune and the cattle. Maeva believes it. The reader has not been shown the harvest.
