# Characters

People the reader can name by the end of *Stolen Breath*. Bodies and wants stay in `canon/characters/`. This file is only how far the book has taken the reader.

## The pair

### Tsunari Thorne

* **Lived.** The theft, the hunt, the first touch, the defection, the barrens, the sump, the Vault, the siege, the compact, and the morning she wakes branded and alone. The reader has watched her measure leverage while pinned, inoculate two soldiers, and break the Forger's staff. They have been in the room for the consummation and the dawn vow.
* **Named.** She is 26, Dromaeon-spliced, uncollared, and the daughter of a man the dome called dead. Her sister's name is on a roster and on a telemetry audit. The Vault shows five harmonics in her blood and calls that the Mosaic Keystone. A local stabilizer made from that blood quiets a lattice. She does not yet hold a cure that travels without her hand.
* **Open.** Whether her father is alive on the far side of the sea, and what the Ghost Beacon actually says. Whether the brand is a mark, a claim, or a tracker. Whether the stabilizer Kira carries will hold in a body Tsunari has not touched.

### Vram Tyage

* **Lived.** The commander who hunts her, lies to Corvus, kills an Inquisitor, falls, burns, and chooses her over the dome and over the Forger. The reader has felt the fever, the silence when her skin is on him, the wings, and the Pyric Crucible on the gantry. The last image is him in chains, taken toward the salt.
* **Named.** Simurgh heat, Gryphon wings, one nape socket, a squad that follows him into mutiny. The Vault tells him the genome is older than Apex. He calls her Tsune in the field. Tsunie, if it lands, is private and not yet the name of the book.
* **Open.** Whether he is awake, willing, or enthroned when the zealots crown him. What the Cathedral of Salt will ask of his body. The kill that can stop his heart has not been fired, and the reader has not seen the hand that holds it.

## The squad

### Cassian Vance

* **Lived.** The reader watches him desert, buy a false ground, and die of the siphon and Lindwurm venom in the Fatal Siphon. The squad is not in that room.
* **Named.** Second, brother, the first to notice Vram protecting the captive.
* **Open.** When Toby and the others learn he is dead. The mutiny in the Vault is written without him.

### Veda Frost and Ferrin Calder

* **Lived.** Banter, the wide shot, the defection, and the inoculation. Their tremors stop in the Great Hall. Ferrin fights beside Boran on the catwalks.
* **Named.** Their strains and their Arkun sit in the dossiers. The book shows the silence, not the full power.
* **Open.** How long a dose holds once Tsunari is gone. Whether the Directorate can still raise the gain on a soldier who has mutinied.

### Toby Vance

* **Lived.** The ice-salve, the slipstream that blocks the flak, the quartz talon-beast, the lowered rifle.
* **Named.** Cassian's younger brother. The carving is a promise, not a flight.
* **Open.** He does not know his brother is dead. The lattice clock on his body is in the dossier, not spent as a scene.

## The Ring

### Ren

* **Named.** The courier who brings them to Chen. A girl in the early rattle of the Choke. She is a stake, not a plotted life.
* **Open.** Whether she lives through the purge, and whether the cure will matter to her in time.

### Boran Vael-Korr

* **Lived.** The rail, the blast door, the insults with Ferrin, the body that takes the hit so the door can shut.
* **Named.** Lindwurm, former heavy breacher, old for a combat chimera.
* **Open.** What the door cost him. He is standing at the end. The book does not say he is whole.

### Madame Vrena Chen

* **Lived.** She prices Vram's old patrols, then vents steam into the purge and keeps the stills and the shelters fed.
* **Named.** Baseline, born before the Storm, chair of the scrubber guild.
* **Open.** How long a guild can feed a ring the dome has decided to write off.

### Kira Brandt

* **Lived.** The crawler, the rescue from the feral pack, the road to Boran's spur. She leaves with encrypted drives of the stabilizer.
* **Named.** Fenris, no corporate pedigree, a road that is not the dome and not the sump.
* **Open.** Whether those drives start anything outside Sector 09. The reader does not see the next enclave.

### Gideon Cross

* **Lived.** The Vault, the Scythian plates, the spectrometer, the stabilizer, the decoders that open the deep layer of the Lazarus Key.
* **Named.** Curator, old, the optometer eye, the un-redacted pact in his keeping.
* **Open.** Whether neutrality survives a siege he hosted. The reader has not watched the guild punish him for taking a side.

## The dome

### Director Elena Corvus

* **Lived.** A voice. She orders the execution, then the crimson purge. Vram lies to her and the lie holds long enough to run.
* **Named.** Apex, Chrysalis-kept, the woman who calls him her weapon. The reader has not sat in her room. The Sterile Room shows the gilded cage through other hands.
* **Open.** Whether she will come in person. Whether the woman on the radio is still the woman who built him.

### Sora Thorne

* **Lived.** One audio log: jasmine, Lab 4, a sister she thinks is dead, a father she thinks is gone, faith that the dome is making a cure.
* **Named.** Twenty, baseline, on the liquidation list once the pylons finish. Her signature is on the audit that hunts Tsunari. They do not meet.
* **Open.** What Sora will do when the list and the sister are the same fact. The reader knows both. Sora knows neither.

### Dr. Jeffrey Thorne

* **Named.** A seed, and only a seed. The Ghost Beacon carries his shorthand from inside the Stygian Ocean. The Vault says he engineered the Keystone. The epigraphs use his journals. The book does not show his face, his landing, or a green shore.
* **Open.** Alive or recorded. The Cradle, the corridor, and a reunion are not in this book's hands.

## The cult

### The Forger

* **Lived.** The offer, the army, the demand that Tsunari burn. Her staff breaks. The cult scatters. The Gospel continues without her in the frame.
* **Named.** The doctrine, the tithe, the brands, the prophecy of a winged deliverer.
* **Open.** Dead, fled, or still giving orders from a sump the siege did not reach.

### Caelia

* **Lived.** Jealousy, ambush, the ducts above the consummation, the radio that calls her bait, the blow she takes for Tsunari, the wounds Tsunari closes.
* **Named.** Ember-Seven, taken at fourteen, promised a king.
* **Open.** What she is after the dogma breaks. Alive, and not yet a companion with a next job.

### Malakar

* **Lived.** The flail, the duel, the melted weapon, the flood that takes him.
* **Named.** Ember-Prime, the conversion the recordings show, pain burned out of him.
* **Open.** The flumes do not return a body. The reader is meant to keep him.

### Sister Maeva

* **Lived.** Only in the Gospel. The brand, the rune, the hum in the rock, the news that Vram is already on the road to the Cathedral of Salt.
* **Open.** Who she answers to now that the Forger's staff is broken.

## Not on this book's page

These people are in the bible. The chapter promise does not spend them. For the reader they are not a rumor yet. They are an absence.

* **Doc Aaron Mercer.** No scene, no tell, no betrayal. The clinic on the author map is not a reveal.
* **Arbiter Lyraen.** No dead-drop, no voice, no argument with Xaevis.
* **Archon Xaevis.** The reader meets Inquisitors and a plan. They do not meet the man who can stop a heart.
