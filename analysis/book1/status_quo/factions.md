# Factions

Who the reader believes is holding power at the end of *Stolen Breath*. The ideology lives in `canon/world/factions/`. Who may command whom is in `canon/world/politics.md`. This file is the depth.

## Lived

* **The Consortium, as a hand from the dome.** Corvus orders a death and then a purge. Chancellor Ward signs the lee back from four thousand people and goes back to dinner. The reader understands the cage: youth and clean air inside, arithmetic outside. They have not seen a board meeting or the other two companies at work.
* **Aeros-Legion 7, as a family that breaks.** Cassian's death, the wide shots, Toby's block, the mutiny after the doses. The reader knows this squad. They do not know the wall garrison, the Iron Division, or the other forts as places with their own day.
* **The Enlightened, as a sump that can still reach a bunker.** The Forger's offer, the Ember Coven, the steam and the flail, the scattering, and then Maeva's liturgy after the staff is broken. The faith is not dead because the priestess lost a fight.
* **Chen's guild, as the reason the ring can still drink.** Stills, shelters, steam used as a weapon. A baseline woman who can tell a commander what he broke.
* **The Curators, as the Vault.** Gideon, the discs, the plates, the spectrometer. Neutrality is the oath in the bible. On the page, the Vault picks a side by opening the door.

## Named

* **Apex GeneSys** as Corvus's house and Sora's lab. **Aethelgard** and **Vanguard** are company names the reader may pass in an epigraph or a patent. They are not characters.
* **Kira's road** as a way out that is not a cell and not a guild. One crawler, one departure.
* **The Vaelen as a sky and a schedule.** Barges, Inquisitors, a fourteen-month plan. The faction file is the species. What the reader has of the species is in `vaelen.md`.

## Open

* Who commands the zealots after the Forger's staff breaks. Maeva acts. The chain of command is not shown.
* Whether the Consortium treats Sector 09 as lost, or as a purge that will be repeated.
* Whether a mutinous squad can stay in the ring without the Directorate burning the gain back up.
* The Preservers, Lyraen, and any Vaelen who disagrees with the schedule. The reader has not been offered a second alien.
* Mercer's cell. The resistance in this book is Chen, Kira, Ren, Boran, and the Vault. The mentor and the betrayal are not on the page.
* Whether the Curators can go back to being untouchable after hosting a siege.
