## Watchlist

`apex` leaves out the company. Apex GeneSys, Apex Bio, Apex corporate, Apex executives, and the dispatch labels account for 11 uses that are not in the table. The 12 below are the plain word (`apex predator`, `at the apex`).

The hundred most-used words in the manuscript end at `then` (98). Four specialized words sit inside that hundred, and they lead the table. `Commander` (103) and `flight` (101) are in that hundred too: the rank, and the wing. `Titanium` (73), `kinetic` (58), and `siphon` (44) are technical, and they fall outside it.

## Coined `ae` and `ea`

Only coined words. English (`vertebrae`, `aerial`, `aerosol`, `algae`) is left out. `Aerie` is the English word for a nest (9, as the High Aerie). `AE-701` is a serial, not a word.

Six coined stems use `ae`. Together they appear 176 times.

`Vael` is Boran Vael-Korr. The hyphen splits the name, so the count is the `Vael` half.

`Lyraen` and `Xaevis` are coined `ae` names in the bible. Neither appears in Book 1.

No coined word in Book 1 uses `ea`. The leash, the Great Cleave, Ghost Beacon, hearth, and breacher are English words used as labels.
