# World & History: The Broken Biosphere (2050)

## 1. Timeline of the Collapse & The Arrival

### 2028–2031: The Thermal Threshold (The Scorching)
*   Global temperatures crossed the irreversible +2.8°C tipping point.
*   The planetary water table collapsed across equatorial belts; agricultural systems failed simultaneously across North America, Eurasia, and Africa.
*   Megacities devolved into rationing zones governed by private security conglomerates.

### 2032: First Contact — The Descent of the Spires
*   Three colossal, non-inertial biomechanical vessels—dubbed *The Spires*—entered Earth's orbit and settled above the dead oceans.
*   The extraterrestrial species, the **Vaelen**, initiated communication not through open broadcast, but through encrypted quantum channels directly to the boards of directors of the world’s twelve largest trans-sovereign conglomerates.
*   **The Pitch:** Terraforming climate-stabilization matrices, clean fusion catalysts, and cellular longevity therapy.
*   **The Price:** Sovereign extraction rights over terrestrial heavy metals, geothermal vents, and absolute diplomatic immunity.

### 2035: The Elysium Accords
*   The world’s political map was dissolved and redrawn. Nation-states were declared obsolete.
*   The **Consortium of Eden** was born: an autocratic coalition of corporate oligarchs, tech dynasties, and Vaelen diplomats.
*   Work began on the **Green Domes**—monumental atmospheric bio-shields built to enclose prime ecological zones.

### 2038–2046: The Great Wall & Abandonment
*   As the Domes were completed, baseline humanity was systematically expelled into the surrounding unshielded terrain, now called the **Gray Sectors** or the **Rust Barrens**.
*   The Consortium ceased all atmospheric scrubbing outside the Domes. The surface air became toxic: an abrasive stew of silica dust, sulfur, and industrial fallout known as the **Amber Haze**.

### 2047–2050: The Atmospheric Phasing (The Current Crisis)
*   The true nature of Vaelen "climate stabilization" became undeniable: they are not saving Earth for humans; they are terraforming it into a high-nitrogen, methane-laced biosphere tailored to their home ecosystem.
*   Outside the Domes, humans are succumbing to **Cellular Asphyxia** (colloquially called *The Choke* or *Glass Lung*).
*   **The Ticking Clock:** According to leaked planetary telemetry, total atmospheric inversion will reach irreversible saturation in **14 to 18 months**. Baseline humanity outside the domes will simply suffocate.

## 2. Planetary Ecology & Atmosphere

### The Atmosphere (The Amber Haze)
*   **Sky Quality:** A permanent copper-tinted twilight. Sunsets are an unnatural, violent violet due to alien atmospheric aerosols deployed from Vaelen terra-pylons.
*   **Respirators & Filter Cartridges:** Clean air is currency in the Gray Sectors. The standard currency unit among scavengers is the *Liter-Hour* of scrubbed O2.
*   **Acid Rain & Glass Winds:** Storms carry fine crystalline particulate that shreds untreated fabric and human skin within hours.

### The Alien Flora: The Spore Invasion
*   Where Vaelen terraforming pylons pulse deep in the earth, terrestrial plant life has been replaced by:
    *   **Phosphor Tendrils:** Bioluminescent vine networks that siphon moisture from dead soil and emit sweet, narcotic neurotoxins.
    *   **Spore Orchids:** Massive carnivorous floral growths that thrive on sulfur and decay, releasing clouds of purple sporocarps that burn human lungs.
    *   **Glass Reeds:** Silicon-based vegetation that chokes rivers and crystallizes standing water into toxic gelatin.

## 4. Key Lore Mysteries to Unravel

1.  **Why do the Vaelen need human DNA at all?** 
    *   *The Twist:* The Vaelen are an ancient, biologically dying species facing genetic stagnation. They cannot reproduce naturally. They need human genomic plasticity to incubate their next evolutionary phase.
2.  **What is the Origin of the Griffin Units?**
    *   They are not born in labs from scratch; they are the strongest, most genetically resilient children kidnapped from the Gray Sectors during early childhood, stripped of memory, and spliced with chimeric apex-avian retroviruses.
3.  **The Hidden Vault:**
    *   Rumors exist of a pre-collapse seed and genome repository ("Project Gaia-Zero") hidden beneath the tectonic bedrock of the northern wastes, containing uncorrupted planetary DNA.
