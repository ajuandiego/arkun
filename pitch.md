# The Storm-Born Cycle: Series & Book Pitches

## Series Logline
In Year 40 AS (forty years After the Storm), Earth belongs to an alien empire that bought the atmosphere from corporate oligarchs and is systematically phasing out human air. When rogue Dromaeon-spliced bio-hacker **Dr. Tsunari Thorne** steals the mathematical proof of humanity's impending extinction, the regime unleashes its supreme weapon: **Commander Vram Tyage**, a Simurgh-spliced soldier burning with a lethal 106°F solar furnace. But when their lethal combat ends in an accidental touch, the screaming chronic agony in Vram's skull goes dead silent. To survive, the predator must defy his creators, ignite a planetary mutiny, and burn the sky to keep her alive.

## Trilogy Book Pitches

### Book 1: *A Spark in the Rust*
*   **The Hook:** A hunted hacker with sickle-claw reflexes. A winged soldier with a burning fever. One touch that changes the fate of Earth.
*   **The Pitch:** Outside the sealed Green Domes, Sector 09's toxic Gray Ring is a powder keg. When Tsunari hacks the courier terminal and steals the *Lazarus Key*, Commander Vram Tyage is sent to eliminate her. But in the ruins of a collapsed solar farm, her touch accidentally grounds his neuro-lattice fever, bringing him peace for the first time in his life. Forced into a volatile rogue alliance after crashing into the feral Rust Barrens, the two lethal combatants must survive wild transgenic packs, defect from an empire, and defend the ancient secrets of The Glass Vault before the Consortium collapses the frontier into dust.

### Book 2: *The Iron Chrysalis*
*   **The Hook:** To save a dying planet, they must cross the burning wastelands. But the deepest enemy wears the face of the family they trust.
*   **The Pitch:** On the run with his defected squadron, Vram wages a guerrilla sky war while Tsunari races to synthesize a global aerosol cure using a rare enzyme gifted by dissident alien preservers. But as paranoia grips the rebellion, Tsunari uncovers the terrifying truth: alien Simulacra—monsters cloaked in human flesh—have infiltrated the highest ranks of the human resistance. When her own mentor betrays them, Vram makes the ultimate sacrifice: holding off an entire army alone so Tsunari can escape, leaving him chained and hauled into the stars aboard the orbital flagship Spire Prime.

### Book 3: *The Unchained Sky*
*   **The Hook:** She will tear down the stars to get him back. He will rise from the ashes to break the sky.
*   **The Pitch:** With humanity's extinction clock counting down its final hours, Tsunari uses the synthesized aerosol cure to awaken hundreds of leashed chimeric soldiers, uniting human survivors and augmented legions into a massive planetary armada. Storming the orbital Spire to rescue Vram from Archon Xaevis’s torture chambers, they fight their way to the atmospheric core. When the master kill-switch flatlines Vram's heart, his mythic solar rebirth sparks the final revolution—reversing the planetary Terra-Pylons, calming the impassable oceans, and shattering the alien's extinction checkmate by leading humanity's great migration toward **The Verdant Cradle**—a hidden green continent where mankind and chimeric kind can rebuild free.
