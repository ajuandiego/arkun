Forty years after the Storm, Earth breathes on borrowed air. The domes belong to the corporations. The sky belongs to the soldiers they grew. Everyone else is learning how to suffocate.

The Arkun Cycle is the story of Dr. Tsunari Thorne and Commander Vram Tyage. She is a rogue hacker built for speed. He is a winged officer burning from a lattice he cannot shut off. He is sent to hunt her. One touch changes the terms.

Three books. One bond.
