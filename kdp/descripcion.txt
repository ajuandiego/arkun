She stole the proof. He was sent to bring back her body.

Outside the sealed domes, air is a debt. Dr. Tsunari Thorne runs the Gray Ring on sickle-claw speed and a throat the regime never collared. She cracks a courier terminal, takes the Lazarus Key, and vanishes into the dust. The Consortium answers with the hunter who does not miss: Commander Vram Tyage, a winged Simurgh whose blood runs at a killing fever and whose orders are drilled into his spine.

In the wreck of a solar farm, her hand finds the port at his throat. The scream in his skull goes silent. For the first time in ten years, the weapon can think. She is the ground. He is the furnace. Neither of them was built to need the other.

Hunted by the legion he used to command, they cross the cold barrens with one rule between them: partners, or nothing. Touch is the only mercy in a world that priced every breath. It may also be the spark that burns the empire down.

Stolen Breath is Book One of The Arkun Cycle, a biopunk romantasy of enemies, forced proximity, and one open-door union earned in fever and blood.
