# Locations & Sensory Worldbuilding

## 1. Eden Dome Alpha (The Gilded Cage)

The capital biome of the Consortium of Eden, housing the high corporate aristocracy and Vaelen diplomatic emissaries.

*   **Visual Atmosphere:**
    *   Vast, vaulted atmospheric shields made of hexagonal photovoltaic glass that bathe the city in perpetual, flattering golden sunlight.
    *   Curving terraces of gene-modified alabaster marble and hanging gardens of genetically altered, thornless crimson roses that bloom in geometric perfection.
    *   Sleek monorails gliding silently on magnetic cushions above crystal-clear canals.
*   **Sensory Profile:**
    *   *Scent:* Overwhelmingly sterile; expensive synthetic cherry blossom perfume designed to mask the faint, bitter metallic tang of recirculated ozone.
    *   *Sound:* The continuous, almost imperceptible low frequency hum of the atmospheric scrubbers; delicate classical chamber music broadcast through public plazas; polite, hushed laughter.
    *   *The Dread Beneath:* An unsettling lack of natural wind, insects, or rain. Everything feels preserved under amber, like specimens pinned in an entomologist's display case.

## 2. Sector 09: The Gray Slums (The Rust Barrens)

The sprawling expanse of collapsed 21st-century urban ruins outside the dome walls, where baseline humans fight for survival.

*   **Visual Atmosphere:**
    *   Skeletal remnants of ancient concrete skyscrapers half-swallowed by dunes of toxic red silica sand.
    *   A chaotic maze of corrugated sheet metal shacks, jury-rigged power cables siphoning static electricity from the Amber Haze, and flickering neon signs advertising recycled water and illicit filter cartridges.
    *   Dusk brings a violent violet sky as the alien atmospheric seeders pulse from orbit.
*   **Sensory Profile:**
    *   *Scent:* Acrid sulfur, burnt plastic, wet rust, and the hot copper taste of airborne dust caught on the back of the throat.
    *   *Sound:* The whistling howl of glass winds slicing through rusted steel frames; the coughs of workers suffering from the Choke; the heavy, rhythmic thud of Consortium patrol drones overhead.

## 3. The High Aerie: Aeros-Legion 7 Base

An airborne staging fortress suspended from the structural trusses of the Dome's northern atmospheric bulkhead, thousands of feet above the earth.

*   **Visual Atmosphere:**
    *   Dark, utilitarian carbon-fiber catwalks suspended over open, dizzying vertical drops.
    *   Amniotic maintenance bays where Roc-class bio-interceptors hang like dormant, chitinous bats from docking harnesses, their organic hulls weeping clear vascular coolant into drainage grates.
    *   The private quarters of the Griffin pilots: stark, minimalist, illuminated by pale cyan status monitors, devoid of personal possessions except for weapon racks and neural calibration cradles.
*   **Sensory Profile:**
    *   *Scent:* Aviation bio-fuel, hydraulic fluid, scorched ozone, and the cold bite of thin, sub-zero high-altitude air.
    *   *Sound:* The roar of jet turbines cycling up; the screech of metal talons locking onto landing pylons; the harsh, clipped military bark of flight controllers.

## 4. Key Forced-Proximity Havens

### The Crashed Roc-Interceptor (The Wreckage)
*   Downed in a desolate crater during their escape.
*   The cockpit is cramped, sealed against a raging acid dust storm outside.
*   The interior smells of warm organic bio-fuel and blood. The life-support heater is failing; the only source of warmth is Kaelen's chimeric body heat. They are trapped shoulder-to-shoulder, listening to the corrosive sand lash against the cockpit canopy.

### The Subterranean Metro Bunker (The Catacombs)
*   A pre-collapse subway station buried eighty feet beneath the desert bedrock.
*   Flickering emergency sodium lights cast long, dramatic shadows across cracked ceramic tiles and rusted train tracks.
*   Water drips into dark puddles. It is cold, damp, and isolated from Consortium scanners—the first place where they can strip off their armor, breathe without respirators, and confront the raw truth of what they are becoming to each other.

### The Foot of Terra-Pylon Seven
*   A monumental biomechanical monolith towering miles into the stratosphere, anchoring the alien terraforming grid.
*   Surrounded by a surreal forest of bioluminescent phosphor tendrils and purple spore orchids.
*   The air here tastes sweet and narcotic; reality feels dreamlike and dangerous.
