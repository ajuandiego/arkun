## Verdict

The revision made the opening honest and the ending almost single. Tsunari now steals an encrypted clock, not the whole planetary proof. Sora's handoff matches in both memories. The Gospel no longer kills the wrong men. Weight is one number, the prowler is an inductive drive, and her eyes on the roof are amber horizontal slits again. What still threatens the book is the last morning: he says he has the watch, and she wakes branded in an empty room with no struggle on the page. Gunships still fly in a world that buried its fuel. Cassian still misses the mutiny. And the book still has two consummations while the canon files still disagree about whether Book 1 may have one.
