# Tech Plates

> **Format:** Ready-to-copy prompts for Gemini / Imagen.  
> **Style:** The same comic sheet as the character plates: bold ink, crosshatch, halftone. No portrait. The point is the machine on a body.  
> **Rule:** These plates follow the canon. The leash is one socket at the nape and two links down the upper back. The Bodkin is one dark knife until it is taken apart. Then the blade and the pointed pommel both come off, and the hidden contacts show at both ends of the grip. A glowing gadget, a battery, or a radio in the grip is wrong.

---

## 1. The Leash on any chimera

The same hardware sits on every corporate chimera. Strain changes the body around it. It does not change the socket.

*   **Idle:** one round socket, centered where the head meets the back. Two short metal links leave its lower edge and run down the upper back. The flesh on either side of the socket lies flat.
*   **Hot:** the carrier is up. The flesh on the left and right sides of the socket is only slightly puffed. The neck stays a normal neck. The links stay metal. They do not swell.

### Prompt
```text
Character hardware sheet in modern western comic book art style, bold ink lineart, crosshatching and halftone dot shading, crisp colors. Plain warm-gray ground. No text, no captions, no labels, no letters, no numbers. No wings. No feathers.

Two views of the same generic adult, seen from behind, hair cropped short so the nape is bare. Neutral skin. No scales, no fur. Frame from the crown to the mid-back. Left view and right view only.

The leash, identical in both views:

1. One round dark-metal socket centered where the head meets the back. A circular port in a small dark seat. One socket only.

2. Exactly two short flat metal links, side by side, leaving the bottom of that socket and ending a hand's length down the upper back. Count them: a left link and a right link. Not a chain. Not one cable. Not a rod down the spine. Not jewelry.

Left view, idle: skin on both sides of the socket is flat and normal.

Right view, hot: the same socket and the same two links. The skin immediately left of the socket and immediately right of the socket is only slightly puffed, a mild swelling. The neck is still a normal neck. Not a lump. Not a goiter. The links are not swollen.

Forbidden: wings, feathers, a third figure, a chain, one single cable, a spine rod, a second port, a collar, a gun, text.
```

*   **Aspect Ratio:** `16:9`
*   **Output:** `08_media/tech_leash_02.jpg`
*   **Recommended Negatives:** `text, captions, typography, labels, letters, numbers, watermark, wings, feathers, chain, single cable, spine rod, second port, collar, gun, goiter, huge swelling, deformed neck, anime, photorealistic, blurry`

## 2. The Bodkin

Match `08_media/bodkin.jpg`. Seated, it is one dark knife. Apart, the hidden contacts show from both ends.

*   **Seated:** a five-inch dark carbon-steel blade, a slim engraved grip, a bright collar where the blade meets the grip, and a faceted pointed pommel. No visible tech.
*   **Apart:** the blade is off one end. The pointed pommel is off the other. Both ends of the grip show dark metal interface contacts. No glow.

### Prompt
```text
Hardware study in modern western comic book art style, bold ink lineart, crosshatching and halftone dot shading, crisp colors. Plain warm-gray ground. No text, no captions, no labels, no letters, no numbers. No person. No hand. No hair.

Two views of the same small knife, drawn large. The whole object, seated, is about the length of a hand. Not a sword.

Left, seated: one dark piece. A five-inch straight carbon-steel blade with a sharp point and a real edge. A slim dark grip with faint engraved lines. A narrow bright metal collar where the blade meets the grip. A faceted pointed pommel at the butt, like a small steel spear-butt, not a flat cap. No seam that reads as a gadget. No glow. No wires. No screen.

Right, taken apart, the same three pieces laid in a row: the blade, the grip, the pointed pommel. The blade is off one end of the grip. The pointed pommel is off the other end. Both exposed ends of the grip show small dark metal interface contacts, rectangular nodes, not a circuit board and not a light. The blade's tang shows the matching contacts. The inside of the pommel is plain metal. No lead clipped on. No glow.

Forbidden: glow, LED, screen, battery, antenna, lightning, a USB stick, a gun, a person, text.
```

*   **Aspect Ratio:** `16:9`
*   **Reference:** `08_media/bodkin.jpg`
*   **Output:** `08_media/tech_bodkin_01.jpg`
*   **Recommended Negatives:** `text, captions, typography, labels, letters, numbers, watermark, person, hand, hair, glow, LED, screen, lightning, antenna, USB, circuit board, gun, sword, flat pommel, anime, photorealistic, blurry`
