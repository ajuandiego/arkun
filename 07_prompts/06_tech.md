# Tech Plates

> **Format:** Ready-to-copy prompts for Gemini / Imagen.  
> **Style:** The same comic sheet as the character plates: bold ink, crosshatch, halftone. No portrait. The point is the machine on a body.  
> **Rule:** These plates follow `01_world/biosystem_and_tech.md` section 2. The leash is one socket at the nape and two links down the upper back. A second port, a flush coin, or a rod down the spine is wrong.

---

## 1. The Leash on any chimera

The same hardware sits on every corporate chimera. Strain changes the body around it. It does not change the socket.

*   **Idle:** one round socket, centered where the head meets the back. Two short metal links leave its lower edge and run down the upper back. The flesh on either side of the socket lies flat.
*   **Hot:** the carrier is up. The flesh on the left and right sides of the socket is only slightly puffed. The neck stays a normal neck. The links stay metal. They do not swell.

### Prompt
```text
Character hardware sheet in modern western comic book art style, bold ink lineart, crosshatching and halftone dot shading, crisp colors. Plain warm-gray ground. No text, no captions, no labels, no letters, no numbers. No wings. No feathers.

Two views of the same generic adult, seen from behind, hair cropped short so the nape is bare. Neutral skin. No scales, no fur. Frame from the crown to the mid-back. Left view and right view only.

The leash, identical in both views:

1. One round dark-metal socket centered where the head meets the back. A circular port in a small dark seat. One socket only.

2. Exactly two short flat metal links, side by side, leaving the bottom of that socket and ending a hand's length down the upper back. Count them: a left link and a right link. Not a chain. Not one cable. Not a rod down the spine. Not jewelry.

Left view, idle: skin on both sides of the socket is flat and normal.

Right view, hot: the same socket and the same two links. The skin immediately left of the socket and immediately right of the socket is only slightly puffed, a mild swelling. The neck is still a normal neck. Not a lump. Not a goiter. The links are not swollen.

Forbidden: wings, feathers, a third figure, a chain, one single cable, a spine rod, a second port, a collar, a gun, text.
```
