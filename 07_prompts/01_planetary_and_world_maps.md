# Cartographic Codex: Planetary & World Map Prompts

> **Format:** Ready-to-copy prompts optimized for Gemini / Imagen 3.  
> **Style Anchor:** Hand-drawn Renaissance manuscript on aged parchment, sepia ink linework, geometric compass/ruler guidelines, and delicate watercolor tinting.

---

## 1. The Planet Earth in Year 18 AS (Orbital Globe Codex)

### Prompt
```text
An authentic Renaissance da Vinci codex astronomical illustration of planet Earth in Year 18 After the Storm, depicted as an orbital celestial globe drawn on aged, weathered vellum parchment. 

The spherical planet is hand-drawn with precise sepia ink linework and graphite shading, framed by visible drafting construction lines, faint geometric compass arcs, celestial coordinate circles, and manual brass ruler markings along the margins. 

On the surface of the globe, the scorched, terraformed continents are rendered in fine cross-hatching. A distinct, swirling equatorial band of toxic Amber Haze is lightly tinted in translucent amber-ocher watercolor. Seven distinct, glowing hexagonal clusters of Green Domes shine against the arid terrain, delicately highlighted in pale emerald-green ink. In high geostationary orbit above the globe, three massive extraterrestrial biomechanical city-ships (The Vaelen Spires) are drawn in exquisite technical detail, connected to the upper atmosphere by faint, dashed trajectory lines and geometric calculation angles.

Surrounding the globe are handwritten cursive technical annotations, Latin astrological and meteorological margin notes, degree scale bars, and a classic Renaissance celestial compass rose. Warm cream and sepia background with antique paper texture, light foxing, and natural deckled parchment edges. Highly detailed, authentic historical sketchbook aesthetic, zero 3D digital render, zero glossy CGI.
```

*   **Aspect Ratio:** `1:1` (Square) or `4:3`
*   **Recommended Negatives:** `photorealistic satellite photo, modern digital 3D globe, glossy rendering, blue oceans, anime, futuristic neon holograms`

## 2. Post-Storm World Map (Planetary Cartographic Codex)

### Prompt
```text
An elaborate 16th-century style Renaissance cartographic map of the post-collapse Earth in Year 18 AS, drawn on a large sheet of antique, tea-stained parchment paper with visible compass lines and drafting grid marks. 

The world map features altered continental landmasses showing eighteen years of alien terraforming: shrunken coastlines, vast desert barrens shaded in dense brown ink cross-hatching, and jagged tectonic scars. The map clearly delineates the seven regional Green Dome Clusters marked with precise geometric hexagonal seals, highlighted with subtle translucent green watercolor washes. Sector 09 and its surrounding Gray Ring are drawn in detailed architectural relief with directional coordinate arrows.

Major global hazard zones are labeled in elegant sepia calligraphy: 'The Torrid Kiln' with flame-hatch symbols, 'The Rust Barrens' with fine stippling, and the locations of the monumental alien Terra-Pylons designated by sharp obelisk glyphs with concentric radiation circles. 

The border of the map is framed by a double-ruled manual brass drafting border with latitude and longitude degree ticks. In the upper corners, detailed pencil illustrations depict hand-held drafting dividers, calipers, and an ornate nautical compass rose with intricate wind lines traversing the map. Antique parchment texture, stained vellum, edge wear, historical archival manuscript aesthetic. No modern computer graphics, no digital satellite imagery.
```

*   **Aspect Ratio:** `16:9` or `3:2` (Landscape)
*   **Recommended Negatives:** `modern GPS map, bright neon colors, modern typography, digital vector art, smooth 3D terrain, plastic textures`

## 3. Continental Sector 09 Regional Projection (Frontier Map)

### Prompt
```text
A detailed Renaissance military cartographer's field map of Sector 09 and the adjacent North American frontier, hand-drawn with graphite pencil and iron-gall ink on aged linen parchment.

The central feature is the monumental Eden Dome Alpha, depicted as an enormous geodesic dome with fine geometric facet lines, tinted in pale watercolor emerald. Encircling the dome's massive outer titanium bulkhead is the sprawling, labyrinthine 'Gray Sector Ring'—drawn with dense, irregular cross-hatched street grids, watchtowers, and aqueduct channels. Outside the perimeter, the map stretches across the desolate 'Rust Barrens', marking abandoned industrial solar farms, dry river canyons, and scavenger trade routes in faded crimson ink.

Manual drafting annotations cover the parchment: brass compass navigation arcs radiating from Sector 09, elevation contour lines sketched with light pencil strokes, scale calipers measuring leagues and kilometers, and handwritten cursive survival notes in the margins ('Respirator required beyond marker 4', 'Dromaeon pack sightings in basalt ruins'). Aged paper with light tea stains, creased folds, and authentic Da Vinci notebook texture. High detail, classical hand-drawn cartography.
```

*   **Aspect Ratio:** `16:9` or `4:3`
*   **Recommended Negatives:** `clean digital vector, modern blueprint blue, glossy UI, satellite overlay, anime`
