# Cartographic Codex: Planetary & World Map Prompts

> **Format:** Ready-to-copy prompts optimized for Gemini / Imagen 3.  
> **Style Anchor:** Hand-drawn Renaissance manuscript on aged parchment, sepia ink linework, geometric compass/ruler guidelines, and delicate watercolor tinting.

---

## 1. The Fractured Earth in Year 40 AS (Orbital Globe Codex)

### Prompt
```text
An authentic Renaissance da Vinci codex astronomical illustration of planet Earth in Year 40 After the Storm, depicted as an orbital celestial globe drawn on aged, weathered vellum parchment. 

The spherical planet is hand-drawn with precise sepia ink linework and graphite shading, framed by visible drafting construction lines, faint geometric compass arcs, celestial coordinate circles, and manual brass ruler markings along the margins. 

The globe illustrates the catastrophic geological results of 'The Great Cleave': Central America has vanished into a wide, dark oceanic breach connecting the Atlantic and Pacific; the Red Sea rift has expanded into an open sea-channel completely severing the African landmass; and pre-collapse coastal plains are drowned under dark waters. On the scorched northern continent, a distinct, swirling equatorial band of toxic Amber Haze is lightly tinted in translucent amber-ocher watercolor. Eden Dome Alpha glows as a delicate, solitary hexagonal jewel in pale emerald-green ink. In high geostationary orbit directly above the northern dome, the extraterrestrial biomechanical city-ship 'Spire Prime' is drawn in exquisite technical detail, connected to the mountaintops by a faint vertical sky-tether line.

Surrounding the globe are handwritten cursive technical annotations, Latin astrological and meteorological margin notes ('Orbis Fractus Post Tempestatem'), degree scale bars, and a classic Renaissance celestial compass rose. Warm cream and sepia background with antique paper texture, light foxing, and natural deckled parchment edges. Highly detailed, authentic historical sketchbook aesthetic, zero 3D digital render, zero glossy CGI.
```

*   **Aspect Ratio:** `1:1` (Square) or `4:3`
*   **Recommended Negatives:** `photorealistic satellite photo, modern digital 3D globe, glossy rendering, intact Central America, blue oceans, anime, futuristic neon holograms`

## 2. The Cleaved World & The Fog of War (Cartographic Codex)

### Prompt
```text
An elaborate 16th-century style Renaissance cartographic map of the fractured post-collapse Earth in Year 40 AS, drawn on a large sheet of antique, tea-stained parchment paper with visible compass lines and drafting grid marks. 

The world map features drastically altered continental landmasses showing the Great Cleave: drowned coastlines, severed continental bridges, and jagged tectonic scars. The northern refuge continent ('The Boreal Redoubt') is drawn in dense, meticulous cartographic detail with mountains, ruined rail lines, and the glowing hexagonal seal of Eden Dome Alpha and Sector 09. Across the vast oceans—labeled in ornate Renaissance calligraphy as 'Mare Stygium' (The Stygian Oceans)—the seas are depicted with violent hand-drawn wave crests, electrical lightning storm glyphs, whirlpool vortexes, and archaic Latin warnings ('Hic Sunt Tempestates Electromagneticae' / 'Nullus Redit').

In the remote southern ocean quadrant, shrouded in faint stippled cloud shading, a faint, dashed navigational corridor is sketched in crimson ink, labeled 'The Ghost Vector of Thorne', leading toward an unmapped, mountainous volcanic caldera labeled 'Terra Ignota / The Verdant Cradle'. 

The border of the map is framed by a double-ruled manual brass drafting border with latitude and longitude degree ticks. In the upper corners, detailed pencil illustrations depict hand-held drafting dividers, calipers, and an ornate nautical compass rose with intricate wind lines traversing the map. Antique parchment texture, stained vellum, edge wear, historical archival manuscript aesthetic. No modern computer graphics, no digital satellite imagery.
```

*   **Aspect Ratio:** `16:9` or `3:2` (Landscape)
*   **Recommended Negatives:** `modern GPS map, bright neon colors, modern typography, digital vector art, smooth 3D terrain, plastic textures, modern world map`

## 3. The Boreal Redoubt & Sector 09 Regional Projection (Frontier Map)

### Prompt
```text
A detailed Renaissance military cartographer's field map of Sector 09 and the surrounding Boreal Redoubt, hand-drawn with graphite pencil and iron-gall ink on aged linen parchment.

The central feature is the monumental Eden Dome Alpha, depicted as an enormous geodesic dome with fine geometric facet lines nestled within an Alpine mountain basin, tinted in pale watercolor emerald. Encircling the dome's massive 100-meter outer titanium bulkhead is the sprawling, labyrinthine 'Gray Sector Ring'—drawn with dense, irregular cross-hatched street grids, subway line tunnels, watchtowers, and aqueduct channels. Outside the perimeter, the map stretches across the desolate 'Rust Barrens', marking abandoned industrial solar mirror fields, dry river canyons, and scavenger trade routes in faded crimson ink.

At the southern edge of the landmass, jagged coastal cliffs drop off into the churning waves of the Stygian Ocean, marked with hazard cross-hatching and an emergency buoy icon. Manual drafting annotations cover the parchment: brass compass navigation arcs radiating from Sector 09, elevation contour lines sketched with light pencil strokes, scale calipers measuring leagues and kilometers, and handwritten cursive survival notes in the margins ('Respirator required beyond marker 4', 'Dromaeon pack stalking grounds in basalt ruins'). Aged paper with light tea stains, creased folds, and authentic Da Vinci notebook texture. High detail, classical hand-drawn cartography.
```

*   **Aspect Ratio:** `16:9` or `4:3`
*   **Recommended Negatives:** `clean digital vector, modern blueprint blue, glossy UI, satellite overlay, anime, modern city`
