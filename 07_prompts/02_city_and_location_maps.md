## 1. Eden Dome Alpha & Sector 09 (Architectural Cutaway & Cross-Section)

### Prompt
```text
An architectural masterplan and cross-sectional elevation drawing in the style of Leonardo da Vinci's notebook studies, depicting Eden Dome Alpha and its surrounding Sector 09 Gray Ring on aged, fibrous parchment.

The central drawing shows a massive structural cutaway: to the left, the interior of the gleaming Eden Dome Alpha with delicate geometric geodesic truss ribbing, soaring corporate spires, lush terraced hydroponic gardens tinted in translucent green watercolor, and the central atmospheric filtration core. Separating the dome from the outside is a colossal 100-meter titanium perimeter bulkhead wall, detailed with mechanical sluice gates, sentry turrets, and exhaust vents.

To the right of the wall, the chaotic, multi-tiered slums of the 'Gray Sector' are sketched in dense, layered cross-hatching—showing stacked shipping container apartments, precarious scaffolding, steam-vent scrubber towers, and crowded bazaars. 

Drawn with graphite pencil, iron-gall sepia ink, and ruler construction guidelines. Faint compass circles indicate blast radii and air filtration ranges. The margins are filled with handwritten technical annotations, structural load diagrams, tensile stress calculation brackets, and scale rulers. Heavy parchment texture with tea stains, creased edges, and authentic Renaissance sketchbook character. No modern CAD renders, no 3D digital modeling.
```

*   **Aspect Ratio:** `16:9` (Wide landscape)
*   **Recommended Negatives:** `modern CAD blueprint, digital wireframe, neon cyberpunk colors, glossy 3D rendering`

## 2. The Glass Vault (Subterranean Codex Schematic)

### Prompt
```text
A Renaissance architectural isometric cutaway diagram of 'The Glass Vault' archive, eighty feet beneath Sector 09, drawn on heavily aged, sepia-toned parchment paper with manual drafting instruments.

The drawing reveals a multi-level subterranean sanctuary carved out of reinforced municipal subway bedrock. The centerpiece is the 'Great Hall of Quartz': towering circular tiers lined with thousands of illuminated optical quartz and synthetic sapphire disc spools, softly tinted in faint pale-blue watercolor. In the lower crypts, rows of cylindrical cryogenic bacterial DNA dewars ('Living Ink') are drawn with technical precision. 

In the center of the hall, wooden drafting desks are depicted with magnifying brass loupes, optical reading prisms, leather-bound manuscripts, and copper pneumatic message tubes running along the vaulted stone ceiling. 

Framed by fine pencil drafting construction marks, compass degree circles, vertical depth measurement scales showing depth below ground level in meters, and handwritten cursive archival notes in sepia ink. Authentic Da Vinci sketchbook aesthetic with paper foxing, watermarks, and worn parchment edges. Highly detailed, contemplative, historical engineering blueprint.
```

*   **Aspect Ratio:** `4:3` or `1:1`
*   **Recommended Negatives:** `sci-fi neon laboratory, modern high-tech server room, glossy 3D graphics, photographic`

## 3. The High Aerie (Aeros-Legion 7 Chimeric Flight Roost & Launch Ledge)

### Prompt
```text
An authentic Renaissance engineering and anatomical manuscript in the style of Leonardo da Vinci's flight studies (Codex on the Flight of Birds), illustrating 'The High Aerie', the high-altitude roost and launch ledge of Aeros-Legion 7, suspended thousands of feet in the air from the northern structural rib of Eden Dome Alpha, drawn on aged vellum parchment.

CRITICAL SUBJECT: The chimeric soldiers fly autonomously on their own large biological wings; there are NO airplanes, NO jets, and NO mechanical vehicles anywhere in the image.

The central drawing shows a breathtaking cantilevered titanium and stone roost protruding from the dome into open air high above cloud level. Perched along the edge of the vertigo-inducing drop-off ledge are chimeric winged soldiers (Aeros-Legion 7) in dynamic athletic poses: several standing at the precipice spreading massive 14-foot feathered wings, others crouched like predatory raptors on stone perches, and one soldier diving head-first off the cantilevered ledge into the abyss with wings tucked in a high-speed aerodynamic stoop.

Surrounding the central architecture are detailed Da Vinci anatomical flight studies:
1. A Vitruvian flight study of a winged soldier with wings fully unfurled, showing the muscular anchor points where the avian scapulae and powerful flight tendons integrate into the human back and spine.
2. Aerodynamic trajectory arcs drawn with brass compass guidelines, calculating gliding ratios, thermal updraft lift vectors, and terminal dive velocities.
3. Inset study of a soldier's perching talons gripping the ledge, and the lightweight high-altitude chest harness and oxygen mask.

Drawn with precise graphite pencil, sepia iron-gall ink cross-hatching, and ruler plumb-lines. Subtle watercolor washes tint the obsidian-gold feathers of the wings and the pale green curve of the dome's structural glass. Marginalia filled with handwritten Renaissance cursive notes, air pressure barometers, and geometric angle brackets. Authentic antique parchment with tea stains, foxing, and worn edges. Masterful hand-drawn anatomical engineering sketchbook.
```

*   **Aspect Ratio:** `16:9` or `3:2` (Landscape)
*   **Recommended Negatives:** `airplane, fighter jet, aircraft, plane, cockpit, propeller, jet engine, feathered airplane, vehicle, runway, modern hangar, CGI, 3D video game render, anime, modern aviation`

## 4. Redoubt Station 14 & The Solar Farm Ruins (Tactical Field Sketch)

### Prompt
```text
A battlefield reconnaissance sketch and tactical terrain study in the style of an 18th-century military engineer's field journal, drawn on rough, stained parchment with graphite pencil and brown sepia ink.

The sketch depicts 'Redoubt Station 14' nestled among the crumbling ruins of an ancient photovoltaic solar farm in the Rust Barrens. Hundreds of shattered, angled solar mirrors rise from the sand dunes like obsidian headstones, shaded with fine diagonal cross-hatching. In the center sits the reinforced concrete bunker of the redoubt, showing blast-damaged armored doors, communication antennae, and perimeter trenches.

Overlaid across the sketch are tactical field markings: circular pencil arcs drawn with a compass indicating defensive firing lines and EMP trap triggers, prevailing wind vector arrows for dust storms, elevation contour lines, and handwritten scout warnings ('Consortium patrol corridor', 'Basalt viper nests'). Soft, subtle watercolor washes of burnt ocher and rust. Weathered paper texture, frayed edges, authentic expeditionary notebook feel.
```

*   **Aspect Ratio:** `16:9` or `4:3`
*   **Recommended Negatives:** `digital minimap, modern satellite photo, clean vector art, saturated vibrant colors`
