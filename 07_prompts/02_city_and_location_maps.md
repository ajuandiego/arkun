## 1. Eden Dome Alpha & Sector 09 (Architectural Cutaway & Cross-Section)

### Prompt
```text
An architectural masterplan and cross-sectional elevation drawing in the style of Leonardo da Vinci's notebook studies, depicting Eden Dome Alpha and its surrounding Sector 09 Gray Ring on aged, fibrous parchment.

The central drawing shows a massive structural cutaway: to the left, the interior of the gleaming Eden Dome Alpha with delicate geometric geodesic truss ribbing, soaring corporate spires, lush terraced hydroponic gardens tinted in translucent green watercolor, and the central atmospheric filtration core. Separating the dome from the outside is a colossal 100-meter titanium perimeter bulkhead wall, detailed with mechanical sluice gates, sentry turrets, and exhaust vents.

To the right of the wall, the chaotic, multi-tiered slums of the 'Gray Sector' are sketched in dense, layered cross-hatching—showing stacked shipping container apartments, precarious scaffolding, steam-vent scrubber towers, and crowded bazaars. 

Drawn with graphite pencil, iron-gall sepia ink, and ruler construction guidelines. Faint compass circles indicate blast radii and air filtration ranges. The margins are filled with handwritten technical annotations, structural load diagrams, tensile stress calculation brackets, and scale rulers. Heavy parchment texture with tea stains, creased edges, and authentic Renaissance sketchbook character. No modern CAD renders, no 3D digital modeling.
```

*   **Aspect Ratio:** `16:9` (Wide landscape)
*   **Recommended Negatives:** `modern CAD blueprint, digital wireframe, neon cyberpunk colors, glossy 3D rendering`

## 5. The Grand Sump Cistern (The Subterranean Temple of The Enlightened)

### Prompt
```text
A monumental Renaissance architectural cross-section and hydraulic study in the style of Leonardo da Vinci and Piranesi's subterranean etchings, depicting 'The Grand Sump Cistern' beneath Sector 09 on antique, water-stained vellum parchment.

The drawing portrays a colossal, vaulted subterranean industrial cathedral sixty feet beneath the Gray Sector slums. Colossal corrugated storm-drainage culverts and riveted iron sluice pipes breach the stone vaulting, pouring torrents of recycled runoff into tiered, circular settling basins below. Suspended over the churning cisterns are cantilevered iron catwalks, chain pulleys, and hanging braziers burning with coal slag.

In the center, upon an elevated stone platform overlooking the waters, sits 'The Forge of Tempering'—the ritual sanctuary of The Enlightened. Sketched in sharp sepia ink is a heavy wrought-iron anvil and blazing crucible furnace where ritual branding irons are heated, surrounded by robed acolytes kneeling in geometric rings on the damp stone flags. At the altar stands the silhouette of The Forger, holding her iron staff overlooking the subterranean congregation.

Detailed drafting annotations throughout:
- Hydraulic flow-rate vectors and water pressure equations calculated with brackets along the drainage flumes.
- Compass arcs showing structural vault curvatures and cantilever weight distributions.
- Marginal cursive notes in brown iron-gall ink detailing sump tithe volumes, sulfur filtration ratios, and cult doctrine ('The Crucible purifies; the water belongs to the Tempered').

Tinted with delicate watercolor washes: faint sulfuric yellow on the water foam, dark oily charcoal along the catwalks, and a dull ember-orange glow radiating from the forge coals. Authentic aged parchment texture with water stains, creases, and rough deckled edges. Brooding, majestic, and historically authentic.
```

*   **Aspect Ratio:** `16:9` or `4:3` (Landscape)
*   **Recommended Negatives:** `sci-fi glowing neon, digital cyberpunk temple, clean 3D render, cartoon, photographic, modern fantasy magic, glowing runes`
