# Novel Blueprint: Sci-Fi / Biopunk Romantasy (Year 40 AS / 2072 CE)

## 1. World Setting & Premise
*   **Year:** Year 40 AS (2072 CE). The planet endures a post-apocalyptic collapse driven by catastrophic climate breakdown and alien atmospheric terraforming. The only habitable sanctuaries are the "Green Domes," controlled by global corporate-states and elitist technocracies.
*   **The Extraterrestrial Factor:** An alien race (the *Karyom*) arrived four decades ago (in 2032 CE), offering terraforming technology and life-extension advances in exchange for Earth's genetic and mineral resources. However, they allied exclusively with the economic elite, forging a superior ruling caste.
*   **The Global Conflict:** Baseline/traditional humans (struggling in desolate "Gray Sectors") face systematic demographic cleansing as the aliens terraform Earth to suit their own biological requirements rather than human needs.

## 2. Mythological / Fantasy Element Reinterpreted (Biopunk)
*   **Mutant Supersoldiers ("Griffin Unit"):** Governments and megacorporations weaponize advanced bioengineering and microbiology to engineer supersoldiers, fusing human DNA with biological sequences inspired by mythical creatures (specifically, **Griffins**).
*   **Griffin Unit Characteristics:** High-speed avian reflexes, retractable nanocarbon claws, superhuman resilience, neural linking with biological combat craft, and ironclad loyalty enforced via an alien neural bio-network.

## 3. Character Archetypes & Romantic Dynamic
*   **She (The Female Lead / Protagonist):** Field scientist and clandestine rebel from the Gray Sectors. Expert in reverse-engineering and cracking alien tech. Fierce, ingenious, lethal out of necessity, and chronically underestimated by the regime.
*   **He (The Love Interest / Antagonist):** Supreme Commander of the Griffin Unit. A living weapon conditioned for absolute obedience—cold, ruthless, and feared. However, his cyber-biological systems begin destabilizing as his dormant consciousness awakens alongside a visceral, primal compulsion toward the protagonist.
*   **Dynamic:** Intense *enemies-to-lovers*, *forced proximity*, suffocating tension (*slow-burn* featuring high emotional stakes, steamy tension, and power plays).

## 4. Main Plot
1.  **Inciting Incident:** The protagonist discovers and extracts an encrypted file proving that the alien terraforming agenda aims for the total extinction of baseline humanity. The regime launches an all-out hunt for her.
2.  **The Capture Twist:** Instead of turning her in for summary execution, the Griffin Commander captures her in secret upon discovering that her neural signature or blood holds the singular key to hacking and breaking the alien bio-network enslaving him and his unit.
3.  **The Escape / Flight:** Forced together against the clock, they embark on a perilous run across a brutal, post-apocalyptic wasteland, relentlessly hunted by both human corporate strike teams and alien overseers.

## 5. Romance Justification & Tension (Romantasy Tropes)
*   **Biological Dependency / Tether:** The supersoldier's bio-augmentations require constant synchronization or physical contact with her to soothe agonizing neural feedback and cellular rejection caused by the failing alien network (a biopunk twist on the fated mates / bonding mark trope).
*   **Obsession & Surrender:** The clash between his ruthless military conditioning and her indomitable defiance sparks volatile friction that spirals into magnetic, possessive attraction. Intimate scenes serve as the ultimate catharsis of emotional surrender, where he proves he would burn down the entire world order to protect her.
