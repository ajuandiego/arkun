# Ren (The Courier)

*   **Role:** An orphaned teenage data-runner who idolizes Tsunari. He has seen the nape. He knows she is unregistered. He has never put that on a slide. The rest of who knows is in `tsunari.md`.
*   **Narrative Function:** Highlights the genuine human stakes of the Gray Sectors. Ren is in the early rattle of the Choke, from years downwind without filtered sleep. The clock is years of worsening breath, not weeks, and it is still the reason the cure matters to her.
*   **Chapter 1:** His day in Sector 09, and the plan for the service door. He sees Tsunari as the person he runs for. He times the long pass. He does not stand at the keypad.
*   **Chapter 2:** He is in her ear on the radio through the tap and the scramble off the service door. He counts the gap. She cuts the link the moment the raven crosses the searchlights, and she tells him to get to Drew and kill his set.
*   **After that:** He goes to Drew. They come looking. She keeps turning them around. The chapters are in `../../story/stolen_breath/breakdown.md`. Drew is in `drew.md`.
