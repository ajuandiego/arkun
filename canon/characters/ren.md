# Ren (The Courier)

*   **Role:** An orphaned teenage data-runner who idolizes Tsunari.
*   **Narrative Function:** Highlights the genuine human stakes of the Gray Sectors. Ren is in the early rattle of the Choke, from years downwind without filtered sleep. The clock is years of worsening breath, not weeks, and it is still the reason the cure matters to her.
*   **Chapter 1:** He is in her ear on the radio through the tap and the scramble out of the duct. He clocked the crawler. He counts the window. She cuts the link the moment the raven crosses the searchlights, and she tells him to get to Drew and kill his set.
*   **After that:** He goes to Drew. They come looking. She keeps turning them around. The chapters are in `../plot/book1/part_1.md` and `../plot/book1/part_3.md`. Drew is in `drew.md`.
