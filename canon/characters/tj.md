# T.J.

*   **Role:** Self-trained software engineer and hardware geek. Baseline. No graft. He never leaves his bunker.
*   **Age:** 29.
*   **The bunker:** His own hole under Sector 09, a room of scavenged screens, cable, and a weather mast he wired himself. This is where Tsunari, Drew, and Ren spend the hours that are not the clinic or a job. He is in the room. He does not come up.
*   **What he knows:** The name Tsune. The scales, because her shoulders are bare when they sit there. He has never seen the nape. He does not know she is unregistered. He does not know the five dishes or what the hand can do to a leash. He does not ask.
*   **What he does:** The desk. Weather on the lee. Dome traffic. A prowler when one paints a gate. He rides the radio and stays put. The player at the board, not the runner in the street.
*   **Appearance:** Baseline, 5'10", thin from a life in a chair. Underground-pale. Dark hair that needs a cut. A headset that lives on one ear. A faded work shirt, sleeves shoved up, no tool belt. Ink on the fingers from cracked ribbon and solder. Bare feet on a concrete floor he never tracks outside.
*   **The early line:** On the channel in Chapter 1 with the silt call and the gate watch. When she cuts the link he stays down and keeps the passive watch. The bearing he gives Drew and Ren is how they know the prowler turned south. He does not follow. The chapters are in `../plot/book1/part_1.md`.
