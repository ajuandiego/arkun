# T.J.

*   **Role:** Self-trained software engineer and hardware geek. Baseline. No graft. He never leaves his bunker.
*   **Age:** 14.
*   **The bunker:** His own hole under Sector 09, a room of scavenged screens, cable, and a weather mast he wired himself because he does not go up. This is where Tsunari, Drew, and Ren spend the hours that are not the clinic or a job. He is the youngest person in the room. He does not come up.
*   **What he knows:** The name Tsune. The scales, because her shoulders are bare when they sit there. He has never seen the nape. He does not know she is unregistered. He does not know the five dishes or what the hand can do to a leash. He does not ask.
*   **What he does:** The desk. Weather on the lee. Dome traffic. A prowler when one paints a gate. He rides the radio and stays put. The player at the board, not the runner in the street.
*   **Appearance:** Black, baseline, 14, still growing, about 5'4", thin from a life in a chair. Dark skin that does not get the dust-tan of the street, because he never goes up. Short hair. A headset that lives on one ear. A faded work shirt too big in the shoulder, sleeves shoved up, no tool belt. Ink on the fingers from cracked ribbon and solder. Bare feet on a concrete floor he never tracks outside.
*   **The early line:** On the channel for the prep and the tap. He does not come up. When she cuts the link in Chapter 2 he stays down and keeps the passive watch. The bearing he gives Drew and Ren is how they know the prowler turned south. He does not follow. The chapters are in `../../story/stolen_breath/breakdown.md`.
