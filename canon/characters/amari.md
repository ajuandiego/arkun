# Amari (Callsign: "Swift")

A Hawk on Vram's ledge. The early Aerie scenes are where she stands.

*   **Role:** Specialist, Aeros-Legion 7. On the roll. She wears the socket.
*   **Age:** 24.
*   **Strain:** Avian, common name Hawk. A patrol wing, narrower than a Raven's. No second graft. The body is in `../world/life/strains.md`. The unit is `../world/factions/legion.md`.
*   **Personality:** Dry, and she counts the wind before she counts the joke. She is not Rook's mouth and she is not Toby's softness. She will say the thing the ledge is avoiding, once, and then she flies it.
*   **Appearance:** 5'8", fair, blonde hair cut short enough for a flight hood, a few strands at the jaw. Grey-blue eyes. A Hawk wing. The vanes are pale wheat, darker along the leading edge. They are not Toby's cream and rust, and they are not Vram's black. Black flight kit, high collar, human hands. The siphon sits at the nape.
*   **Where she is:** On the launch ledge in Chapter 2 with Cassian, Rook, and Toby. In the chapter after the lie, she is in the room when Cassian tries to make the report make sense. She does not know the report is false.
