## 3. Psychological Architecture

### The Ghost (The Backstory Trauma)
When Tsunari was 17 (in Year 31 AS), the Amber Haze was already failing her. Her father, Dr. Jeffrey Thorne, an elite geneticist on the archeo-genetic excavations who secretly collaborated with the undercity Scribes, had learned the Karyon program well enough to isolate the old sequence in the raptor archive. He ran the five mutagens against each other and found a formula that would let one body hold all five facets. He called that formula the Keystone Archetype. The page calls the resulting architecture the Mosaic Keystone. The species history is in `canon/world/factions/karyon.md`. Knowing the haze would not give him a second candidate, and knowing the raid was coming, he inoculated her with that genome and with an Amber Haze antibody culture. The Raptor took. The illness stopped. The other four facets stayed dormant. To protect his daughters, Jeffrey drew the corporate strike team into the deep wastes in an amphibious crawler and plunged into the Stygian Ocean (where he survived to reach the Verdant Cradle). Tsunari escaped into Sector 09, guided and monitored by her father's colleague, "Doc" Mercer. Mercer steered the work. He did not write the formula.

### Before Chapter 1
Nine years in the Ring. She is not a lone scavenger and she does not keep a gang. Mercer is the clinic and the mentor. Drew grew up in that clinic with her: a baseline mechanic, no graft, and the one person besides Mercer who has seen the nape. Ren is the runner she trusts. A few other mechanics know the name Tsune and do not know her throat. She eats at the market and sleeps near the clinic. Wild Raptors are animals, not a crew. Scrap-gangs are a hazard on the canals. The Enlightened are worse if they ever see the scales. Drew is in `drew.md`.

### What she was told
She was seventeen, the haze was already in her, and the raid was coming. Jeffrey named the body that was waking, because she had to live in it and hide it. He told her the Raptor: cool rest, slate scales on the shoulders and the arms, the membranes, the clicks, the stronger legs. Cool skin will pull heat off a warmer body. It will not fix anyone. Hide the nape. Keep her blood off a company slide. The culture stopped the illness. She has lived in that body since she was seventeen. She knows it.

He did not tell her what she was worth. Five facets in one uncollared body is a vivisection warrant if she can say it. She was too young to carry that, and the words were too dangerous to leave in a girl the strike team might take. He never said Keystone. He never said the other four loci. He never said her hand could quiet a leash.

What he left is memory, and it does not assemble before the Vault.

*   A bench sentence he made her repeat until it was cold: "If it cannot go home, it cooks." She filed it as a bad culture.
*   Five dishes on the bench. He counted them and would not name four.
*   Mercer would finish her education. Mercer would not get the notes.

The grapple makes the sentence mean a person. She still cannot finish the count. The spectrometer in the Glass Vault is the five crests, and that is when the dishes get their names. The ground itself is in `canon/world/life/leash.md`.

### The Lie She Believes
> *"Attachment is a trap. The only truth in this world is kinetic speed, calculating distance, and striking before they cage you."*

### The Internal Want vs. Need
*   **What She Wants:** Absolute independence and survival: to decode the Karyon atmospheric keys, liberate the Gray Sectors from corporate control, and remain unbound by any master.
*   **What She Needs:** A peer who learns what her hand can do and leaves the choice of it with her. His wing beside hers is welcome. A keeper is a second collar.

## 7. Romantic Dynamics & Friction Points with the MMC
*   **Initial Stance:** Deadly predator rivalry. When Vram tracks her down in Chapter 1, it is a ferocious clash of primal titans: the blinding solar radiance of the Flight Commander against the hyper-agile shadow-stalking of the Scythe-Stalker Infiltrator.
*   **The Turning Point:** Seeing Vram's body burning in lattice fever. When she touches his bare collarbone to restrain him, two things happen. Her cool Raptor skin takes the heat, and that part she was taught. His mesh goes silent, because the contact closed a path she was not taught. The old bench sentence comes up and does not finish it. She is as frightened as he is. She wants to understand the quiet with him. The rule is in `canon/world/life/leash.md`.
*   **Why she loves him:** His love starts in the silence and then reaches her. Hers starts when he can sell that silence and will not. Sorrow for the draft and the leash revises the sky-lord. She did not know a commander was a taken child, or that the scream is the job. That sorrow covers his squad. It does not pick him. Needing his wing is the early math: he knows her face, the theft, and the quiet, so a man who can sell her has to be kept close. She starts loving him when the sale is in his hand and he lies to Corvus, and when he would rather burn than take a touch she has not given. He fights at her speed and spends the heat where it keeps someone else breathing. She can stand next to that and keep the knife. *Partners, or nothing* is the form she can say. The phases are in `canon/romance/romance_engine.md`.
*   **The Power Dynamic (Fire and Shadow):** He has the wing and the furnace. She has the speed, the knife, and the hand that quiets one mesh when she aims it. They match.

## 8. The Revelation of the Keystone (Book 1 Arc)
She has known since she was seventeen that her father gave her a Raptor body so the haze would stop. In the Glass Vault, when she runs her blood through Gideon Cross's quartz spectrometer, the laser refracts into **five distinct harmonic wave-crests**. The Raptor is the one he named. The other four are the dishes he would not name. She wears no collar. A touch can quiet one mesh she aims at. It does not free the cohort. That later release is a synthesis, not a hand.
