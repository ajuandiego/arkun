# Sergeant Veda Frost (Callsign: "Screech")

*   **Role:** Heavy weapons and close-quarters vanguard. Spliced Galvan and Raptor.
*   **Personality:** Sarcastic, lethal, aggressive. Has a dark sense of humor to cope with the constant neuro-pain.
*   **Appearance:** 5'10", lean, the strength in the legs. No wings. Short-cropped black hair, shaved close at the sides. A hard square jaw and a narrow mouth. Fair skin, so the marks read. Dark irregular spots on the shoulders, the arms, the upper back, and a scatter on the neck and the right cheek. The iris glows yellow. Round pupils. The mouth is hers. Thicker calves than the rest of her frame, with a fine scatter of scales on the shins: that is the Raptor share. The skin carries a wet sheen. The siphon sits at the nape. She is on the legion roll. A sleeveless black legion top, cargo trousers, and boots she can kick off at a wet gate. Fingerless gloves, pulled when the palms are working. A sniper carbine.
*   **Strain Chart & Arkun:**
    *   *Genetics:* **60% Galvan (Blinder) + 40% Raptor.** The grafts are in `../world/life/strains.md`. Galvan sets the body: the spots, the glowing iris, the wet skin, and the palmar discharge. The Raptor share is the legs.
    *   *True Character / Core Drive:* *Unapologetic Defiance / The Cage-Breaker.* Channeling rage into destructive kinetic speed; refuses to bow or beg.
    *   *Manifested Arkun, Blinder:* A Galvan whiteout through the voice and the forearms, thrown while she vaults. It shatters a sensor pack and bursts an unprotected ear. It does not write a memory.
*   **Dynamic with Tsunari:** Initially mocks Tsunari as an un-augmented "mayfly" (baseline human with a short lifespan), but gains deep respect for Tsunari's nerve when Tsunari field-stitches an open wound without flinching.
