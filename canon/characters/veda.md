# Sergeant Veda Frost (Callsign: "Screech")

*   **Role:** Heavy weapons and close-quarters vanguard. Spliced Galvan and Raptor.
*   **Personality:** Sarcastic, lethal, aggressive. Has a dark sense of humor to cope with the constant neuro-pain.
*   **Strain Chart & Arkun:**
    *   *Genetics:* **60% Galvan (Blinder) + 40% Raptor.** A palmar and vocal discharge that whites out ears and sensors, merged with a Raptor's legs and close-quarters talons.
    *   *True Character / Core Drive:* *Unapologetic Defiance / The Cage-Breaker.* Channeling rage into destructive kinetic speed; refuses to bow or beg.
    *   *Manifested Arkun, Blinder:* A Galvan whiteout through the voice and the forearms, thrown while she vaults. It shatters a sensor pack and bursts an unprotected ear. It does not write a memory.
*   **Dynamic with Tsunari:** Initially mocks Tsunari as an un-augmented "mayfly" (baseline human with a short lifespan), but gains deep respect for Tsunari's nerve when Tsunari field-stitches an open wound without flinching.
