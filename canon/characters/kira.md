# Kira Brandt

*   **Role:** Leader of the "Overground Railroad," a clandestine smuggling convoy operating modified diesel-electric sand-crawlers across the toxic Rust Barrens.
*   **Age:** 27.
*   **Strain Chart & Arkun:**
    *   *Genetics:* **85% Lycan (Wolf) + 15% Baseline.** Unregistered. Bare nape, no socket. The graft is in `../world/life/strains.md`: her own hair thickens into fur over the shoulders, the upper arms, and the upper back. Round pupils. Upper canines. Human hands and feet. No claws. *(No corporate pedigree. Lupine-A String, common name Wolf.)*
    *   *True Character / Core Drive:* *The Wild Outlaw / Pack Loyalty.* Rejects both the corporate domes and the cult sumps; lives only for the freedom of the open desert and the survival of her scavenger clan.
    *   *Manifested Arkun, Primal Pack-Sense (Scent-Wake):* Can track molecular scent trails through howling dust storms over twenty miles, detecting approaching drone squadrons and toxic gas pockets hours before electronic radar registers them.
*   **Appearance:** 5'8". An athletic woman: a visible waist, hips, and chest, a woman's neck and jaw, strong legs. Sun-darkened skin, dust in the creases. Long dark hair, nearly black, worn loose. It becomes the same dark fur on the shoulders, the upper arms, and the upper back. The forearms and the chest stay skin. Amber eyes, round pupils, a tapetum in low light. The upper canines show when she talks. A pale scar cuts the left brow and the cheek under it. A fitted strap shirt, cargo trousers, a belt of pouches, bare feet on the crawler deck. In the open barrens she pulls a fur-lined hood up. Grease on the hands.
*   **Personality:** Direct, sharp, and loyal after a price has been paid and a word kept. She does not stop a crawler for a stranger.
*   **Why she stops:** The depot is a cache on her run, and the dogs have been taking it. She rolls in to clear her own yard. What she finds is an uncollared woman, which gets a look because Kira's own nape is bare, and a collared Raven hot enough to pull a satellite onto her crawler. The gun stays up.
*   **The price:** A copy of the schedule skin. Her freight is medicine and people a sweep is hunting. If the lee over Sector 09 dies in 14 months, the road dies with it. She will carry that page to the settlements that pay her. She is not joining a cell. Masking his heat is the same trade she sells every unregistered body she moves. They pay for it. Boran's door is a debt she already owes him. Two fugitives and a real page discharge it. He still decides whether the bulkhead opens.
*   **Plot Function:** Clears the depot because it is hers. Hauls them only after the copy is in her hand. Teaches the heat-mask as a sold trick. Spends the debt that opens Boran's rail. Later she carries that page into the deep wastes because the settlements on her road paid to know the date. The arrival is in `../plot/book1/part_3.md`.
