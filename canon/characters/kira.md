# Kira Brandt

*   **Role:** Leader of the "Overground Railroad," a clandestine smuggling convoy operating modified diesel-electric sand-crawlers across the toxic Rust Barrens.
*   **Age:** 27.
*   **Strain Chart & Arkun:**

The forearms and the chest stay mostly skin. Amber eyes, round pupils, a nocturnal tapetum. Upper canines that show when the lips part. A tracker's nose. An athletic woman: a visible waist, hips, and chest, a woman's neck and jaw, strong legs. Human hands and human feet. No claws. The locked sheet is `assets/media/kira_4.jpg`. *(Note: Strictly no corporate pedigree. Lupine-A String, common name Wolf.)*
    *   *True Character / Core Drive:* *The Wild Outlaw / Pack Loyalty.* Rejects both the corporate domes and the cult sumps; lives only for the freedom of the open desert and the survival of her scavenger clan.
    *   *Manifested Arkun, Primal Pack-Sense (Scent-Wake):* Can track molecular scent trails through howling dust storms over twenty miles, detecting approaching drone squadrons and toxic gas pockets hours before electronic radar registers them.
*   **Personality & Appearance:** Dust-bitten. A fitted strap shirt, cargo trousers, and a belt of pouches. Bare feet. The fur at her shoulders and upper back is visible. Direct, sharp, and fiercely loyal to anyone who shares water and keeps their word. The build is in the strain chart above.
*   **Plot Function:** Rescues Tsunari and the downed Vram from feral Raptors at the abandoned transit depot in Chapter 13; teaches Tsunari how to mask Vram's 106°F thermal flare against orbital satellites; connects the fugitive duo to Boran's underground rail spur; in Chapter 24, carries encrypted copies of the *Lazarus Key* into the deep wastes to ignite resistance enclaves globally.
