# Lieutenant Cassian Vance (Callsign: "Talon-Two")

The soldiers under Vram's command are a family bound by the leash. When he defects, their loyalties split. Cassian is his second.

*   **Role:** Vram's second-in-command and closest thing to a brother.
*   **Age:** 28. Toby's older brother.
*   **Personality:** Cynical, observant, weary. Where Vram is iron and rage, Cassian is smoke and calculation.
*   **Appearance:** 6'1", leaner than Vram. Warm tan skin, a longer face, a day's stubble, a nose broken once and set straight. Short black hair, cropped close, no topknot. Hazel eyes, the same as Toby's, narrower and usually bloodshot when the leash is loud. A Hawk wing, narrower than a Raven's. His vanes are dark slate with a pale grey trailing edge. They are not black, and they do not take Vram's sheen. The Lycan share is the nose and the hearing. He scents a room before he looks. No fur. A black flight coat with a high collar, kept closed, over the legion kit. In the sump he throws an oil-stained mechanic's duster over the wings. Human hands. The siphon sits at the nape.
*   **Strain Chart & Arkun:**
    *   *Genetics:* **70% Avian (Hawk) + 30% Lycan.** The grafts are in `../world/life/strains.md`. A patrol wing, plus a wolf's nose and hearing.
    *   *True Character / Core Drive:* *The Calculating Ghost / Silent Preserver.* Believes survival requires staying invisible and calculating retreat vectors before entering a room.
    *   *Manifested Arkun, Thermodynamic Mirage (Phantom Dive):* Blends visual, thermal, and radar signatures into the surrounding ambient background, leaving ghost after-images that misdirect anti-air tracking and heat-seeking ordnance.
*   **The Conflict:** Cassian is the first to notice Vram's erratic biometric signals and his obsessive protection of the human captive. Cassian is terrified of corporate retribution and clings to doctrine because he fears the siphon. When Vram defects, the Directorate dials the squad's siphon frequency to maximum gain.
*   **Arc:** After Vram goes rogue, the Directorate drives the squad's carrier to the top of what the mesh can carry. Cassian deserts into Sector 09. The static does not drop. In the Fatal Siphon the gain passes what his nerves can hold, and the mesh kills him. No Galvan is in that death. Toby and the squad do not know. That is what waits for a legionnaire without the Mosaic Keystone.
