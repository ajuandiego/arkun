# Arbiter Lyraen

*   **Role:** Chief Genetic Arbiter of the Karyon Spire Citadel; covert leader of the Preserver movement.
*   **Appearance & Presence:** A human frame, taller and more slender than Archon Xaevis: narrower shoulders, a longer neck, finer hands. Bare scalp, cool pale skin. Faint veins at the temples, the throat above the collar, and the inner wrists, pale silver into deep indigo. They are faint at rest and clearer when the eye fractures. A harmonic rides under the voice. She wears the Spire cloth, cut to her frame. The locked sheet is `assets/media/lyraen.jpg`. The body rule is the Anatomy in `../world/factions/karyon.md`.
*   **Motivation & Backstory:** She was among the researchers on the antiquity trials, and she has been building her half of a ground since she helped design the lattice. The project, the Silence, and the enzyme are in `../world/factions/karyon.md`. She is saving the living bench. The old account, that she needs humanity as an emotional cure for a sterile species, loses to that file.
*   **The Double-Agent Dynamic:**
    *   Lyraen cannot openly oppose Xaevis without being purged by the Spire's collective neural mesh.
    *   Lyraen secretly feeds encrypted databursts, terra-pylon override frequencies, and ancient chimeric genomic maps to Tsunari via dead-drops in the ruins.
    *   *The Conflict:* Neither Tsunari nor Vram fully trusts Lyraen at first. They wonder if the alien is simply using them as pawns in an extraterrestrial power struggle.

Where this sheet disagrees with `../world/factions/karyon.md`, that file wins.
