# Boran "The Bastion" Vael-Korr

*   **Role:** Former Sergeant of the 4th Heavy Breachers; veteran subterranean freight line guard and demolition expert.
*   **Age:** 36 (an ancient survivor among combat chimeras).
*   **Strain Chart & Arkun:**
    *   *Genetics:* **100% Ceraton (Rhino).** The graft is in `../world/life/strains.md`: a brow horn, and grey cracked hide over the face, the neck, the shoulders, the arms, and the upper back. The plates stop shrapnel and hold heat. He is not immune to bad air.
    *   *True Character / Core Drive:* *The Immovable Bulwark / The Cynical Protector.* Has seen every corporate promise rot into lies; believes the only truth in the universe is holding the ground with your own two feet.
    *   *Manifested Arkun, Seismic Bastion:* Slams his fists or knuckles into solid bedrock or reinforced decking to project a dense kinetic shock-wave that shatters vehicle axles, halts incoming ballistic charges, and anchors structures from collapsing.
*   **Appearance:** 6'8", wide as a door, a short thick neck. Bald. A heavy horn, the large end of the class, centered on the brow. Grey cracked hide covers most of the face, the neck, the shoulders, both arms, and the upper back. An old crack runs through the plate on his left cheek. The chest and the belly are a deep weathered brown, the skin the hide did not take. Small deep-set amber eyes under the horn. A heavy jaw. No shirt in the tunnels. Dark work trousers, a broad belt, scuffed steel-toe boots, and a demolition chisel on the belt.
*   **Personality & Voice:** Deep, rumbling baritone. Dry, deadpan, and unshakeable.
*   **Inter-Strain Rivalry & Banter:** Harbors a deep contempt for "fragile flyers with hollow bones who run when the wind blows." He and Ferrin Calder trade barbs throughout combat, yet Boran uses the plates to shield Ferrin and Toby from heavy ordnance.
*   **Narrative Function:** Guides Vram and Tsunari through the pre-collapse subterranean heavy-rail conduits beneath the Rust Barrens; holds the blast door against Malakar's pneumatic rail-flail in Chapter 22, taking brutal structural punishment so the cure can be secured.
