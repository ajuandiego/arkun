# Archon Xaevis

*   **Role:** Supreme extraterrestrial coordinator of the planetary terraforming initiative.
*   **Presence:** Elegant, chillingly serene. Bare scalp, pale skin, human ears and nose. At rest the iris is pale and human. The fractured eye on the plate is shock. A harmonic rides under the words. Never raises his voice. Never displays anger. He wears the Spire cloth. The locked sheet is `assets/media/xaevis.jpg`. The body rule is the Anatomy in `../world/factions/karyon.md`.
*   **Motive:** He views baseline humans as obsolete biomass, and chimeric soldiers as animals that can be put down the moment they bite. What he intends for the sky, and for the old sequence, is in `../world/factions/karyon.md`.
*   **Threat Level:** Holds the master kill-switch to the Synapse Lattice in his ceremonial bio-staff.
