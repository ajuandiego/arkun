# World

History, air, the map, and who holds power. Bodies and the leash live under `life/`.

* `calendar.md`: Year 0, the Storm, and the modern timeline.
* `air.md`: pylons, the Amber Haze, the Choke, and the 14-month theft.
* `society.md`: who lives where, the child tithe, and a day inside or outside the wall.
* `geography.md`: the bowl, the sectors, the distances, and the barrens.
* `cradle.md`: the Cradle, the ghost beacon, the Spires, and the migration. Later books.
* `politics.md`: who may order whom, and the alignment of the war.
* `mysteries.md`: open questions from the older notes. The Vaelen motive in that file loses to `factions/vaelen.md`.
* `factions/`: each power, one file.
* `life/`: strains, the leash, Arkun, tools, food, and the crucible serum.
