# The Leash

## 2. The Leash: The Karyon Synapse Lattice

Soldiers call the whole control system the Leash. The Karyon name for the same system is the Synapse Lattice. This section is the rule for the socket, the tab, and a ground. A later line that lets any human hand quiet a siphon loses to this one.

*   **What you see:**
    *   *The Silver Spine:* The mesh inside. A filament net grown through the brainstem and down the cord. It is not a rod on the back, and it is not a pale thread drawn between two ports. The socket and the short tab under it are the part that shows.
    *   *The Siphon:* One interface. A round iris in a dark metal seat, centered where the head meets the back. It is a jack, not a collar and not a coin flush with the skin. A short metal tab leaves the lower edge of that seat. Idle, the metal is cold and the skin around it lies flat. The tab is the same on every registered chimera. It is not a second socket, and it does not swell. When the carrier flares, the skin around the socket reddens. That redness is the inflammation. The metal stays metal. A Raven's set-point feeds that local current, so the socket reddens faster on him than on a Hawk. Pulling the socket out does not free the soldier. The tab and the mesh still ring until the carrier is grounded or the mesh is dead. The locked plate is `assets/media/the_leash_2.jpg`.
    *   *Who wears it:* The socket is fitted when a chimera is entered on a company roll. That person is registered, and the carrier can find them. An unregistered chimera has the graft and a bare nape. No socket, no tab, no carrier. In Sector 09 a bare nape on a body that shows a strain is a vivisection mark. Kira Brandt was never on a roll. Boran Vael-Korr left the 4th Heavy Breachers and is unregistered. Tsunari Thorne is unregistered, and the Keystone is the further fact, below. Veda Frost, Cassian, Rook, Toby, and Vram are on a roll. The hardware is the same.
    *   *The Carrier:* The strain broadcast. Spire Prime radiates it, tuned to that soldier's strain, through this one socket. Gain, the diagnostic, the obey impulse, and the kill ride this wave and no other. There is no battery in the neck.
*   **The tactical link:** The other tuning in the same socket. Voice, the position fix, wind, and target marks. A jaw-key in the field, or a shortwave microphone in a prowler and a bunker. The Directorate hears this link. It does not hear a conversation held with the link shut. It does not see through the eyes, and it does not read a thought. The Keystone does not match this tuning, so aiming the ground does not sink it. He can hold her and lie to Cassian. He can stand in the same room and tell Corvus the asset is dead.
*   **What can be forced:**
    *   *The diagnostic:* An hourly sting through the mesh. Soldiers describe a needle behind the eye. It is a check, not a punishment.
    *   *The obey impulse:* A micro-shock and a conditioned order. It pushes. It does not puppet the limbs. The soldier still flies their own body.
    *   *The gain:* The Directorate can raise the carrier on a cohort it owns. Pain climbs. Lattice Burn accelerates. This is the scream Cassian's squad suffers when Vram leaves the formation.
    *   *The kill:* Archon Xaevis alone. The master carrier stops the heart. Flatline, not a cooked skull. A corporate director cannot fire it.
*   **Tracking:** The position fix is data. It stays up on a soldier she is grounding. Inside the Ring and the Aerie it is good. Pylon static and the open barrens dirty it. They still get a last position from the fix itself. What drops, on that one soldier, is only the strain return. The dot remains. The voice remains. A quiet strain line beside a live fix is the gap they do not yet know how to name. He can blame an EMP. When she releases the aim, the strain return climbs again. A spoken order on the tactical link is not a strain command. Wings, eyes, and the Iron Division can hunt a body the fix already shows. A prowler's beacon is a third machine, in the vehicle. Quieting the strain wave does not hide her. Grounding the soldier does not kill the beacon or the tactical link.
*   **Legion kit that rides the leash:** The flight harness, cut so the nape socket and the tab stay clear, and cut for the wings. The tactical link. Ice-salve for the reddened skin around the socket. High-altitude breathing rigs, which are for thin air at the Aerie and are not the leash. Pyro-gel, which feeds the furnace that also heats the mesh. There is no second tracker bead. The data fix in the siphon is the body tracker. The strain wave is not.
*   **Lattice Burn:** The mesh was insulated from the human nervous system on purpose, so the carrier would return to the Spire and not into the soldier. The waste of that insulation cooks the brain. After years of service the burn becomes chronic: fever, tremor, migraine, and a death that arrives around the early thirties if nothing shunts the carrier. Recalibration doses, rationed by the Karyon, turn the gain down. They do not remove the mesh. The damper is meant to blunt fear and hesitation. It does not delete attachment. Attachment that survives the damper is the mutiny the company fears.
*   **What a ground is:** The strain carrier is a circuit. It must return to the Spire. Tsunari's Mosaic Keystone is the missing return path for that wave only. The path stays shut until she directs it at one soldier. A collared squad standing around her feels nothing. She chooses the mesh. Skin closes the circuit she has aimed: the wave dumps into her and dies in that soldier. The scream stops in him. The redness around his socket fades. The fever that belonged to the leash drops. Voice and the position fix do not. The socket and the tab remain. When she releases the aim, or the contact breaks, the Spire path wins again and the burn returns. Grounding is not removal. The cure that lets a mesh hold a null without her hand is a later synthesis from her blood. It is not what a touch does. Her cool resting temperature is the Raptor body, in `strains.md`. That cool moves across skin whether or not she is grounding him. The cool does not shunt the carrier.

## 4. The Biological Resonance Factor (Synergy over Leash)

Null-Resonance is not a fallout accident and not a property of human skin. Jeffrey Thorne built it into the Mosaic Keystone when he inoculated Tsunari at seventeen. If any bare hand could quiet a siphon, the legion would already be free, and the premise of the book would be a known street trick.

*   **Why it exists:** A corporate lattice is tuned to one strain and insulated from the body so the carrier returns to the Spire. Thorne gave his daughter the expressed body of a Raptor and, in the non-coding DNA, dormant receptor loci for the other four strains. Those receptors recognize every corporate mesh as kin. They do not radiate. They answer when she directs them at one soldier. He gave her no collar. Her nervous system is the return path the mesh was denied: a null. A ground.
*   **The first time:** She was taught the Raptor body. She was not taught this path. What she remembers is in `../../characters/tsunari.md`. In the grapple the contact and the shock close it on him. She is as surprised as he is. After that she can aim. Aim takes attention. A hand on Vram leaves the soldier beside him screaming.
*   **What contact does:** Skin closes the strain circuit she has aimed. That wave shunts into her and collapses in that soldier. Silence, the redness around his socket fading, the leash-fever dropping. A strain command coming down dies with it: diagnostic, obey, gain, and the kill. A spoken order does not. The quench is strongest with a hand on the socket, because that metal is the antenna. A palm on the shoulder still closes it, if the aim is on him. The mesh, the socket, and the tab remain. The tactical link stays live, which is why he can key it with his jaw while her skin is on his.
*   **What nearness does:** Nothing. There is no radius. A leash she is not aiming at stays loud. The Silence Lyraen reads is the return on the one soldier Tsunari is grounding. The position fix on that soldier stays.
*   **What does not ground a lattice:** Another chimera is a second antenna. Baseline skin is insulation. Medics, lovers, and alley remedies have been tried. A human palm fails the same way. The Fatal Siphon is that proof. The Directorate holds Cassian's gain at the top of what the mesh can carry. No hand stops it. The burn takes him.
*   **Any class, one woman:** Because the dormant loci match all five meshes, her hand grounds an Avian, a Lycan, a Ceraton, a Galvan, or a Raptor. It does not have to be Vram. He is the soldier the book follows.
*   **The Tether is the choice after the ground:** Held willingly, and only while she keeps the aim on him, his output feeds her speed and her sink keeps his furnace from cooking the mesh. The cool of her skin is the Raptor body and is there whenever they touch. The glossary names the chosen ground the Biological Tether. It is not a chain. It does not replace the leash hardware. It is the reason a grounded soldier becomes more than a quiet weapon, and it is still a decision both of them make.
