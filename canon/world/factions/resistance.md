# The Resistance

## 4. The Dustborn Resistance & Undercity Networks

*   **Territory:** The collapsed, half-buried mega-cities, subterranean subway networks, and industrial sumps of the Gray Sectors.
*   **The "Curated Terrarium" Reality:**
    *   The Null-Circuit was not an undetected underground movement. Because **Doc Aaron Mercer was a deep-cover Vaelen Simulacrum**, the Spire deliberately allowed the rebel cell to operate.
    *   The harsh, unshielded survival conditions of the Gray Sectors were used as an evolutionary incubator to force Tsunari into synthesizing the genetic cure the dying alien race required.
*   **The Essential Logistics & Autonomous Coalitions:**
    1.  **The Air Scrubber Guild & The Iron Market (Madame Vrena Chen):** The lifeblood of Sector 09's baseline population, and the licensed broker at the gate. The guild keeps the dawn stills, the Night Freeze shelters, the civilian defense net, the market charter, and the certified filter counter. A sleep cartridge costs about a day's wages: expensive, and something a steady worker can buy. It buys easier nights and more years. It does not buy the right to survive until morning. Black-market cartridges undercut her at half the price and half the life. Her night watch keeps scrap-gangs out of the bazaar and does not fight the Iron Division. While suspicious of chimeric soldiers, her alliance with Tsunari supplies water, shelter, filters, and the infrastructure to withstand corporate purges. Her brass mask is the best cartridge in the Ring, and it is also her office.
    2.  **The Overground Railroad (Kira Brandt):** Clandestine Fenris-led desert smuggling convoys operating modified diesel-electric sand-crawlers across the Rust Barrens, linking isolated settlements, moving what the gate taxes too hard (medicine, chips, and people a sweep is hunting), and evading orbital sensor nets.
*   **Crime in the Ring, in three layers:** Scrap-gangs rob caravans and unregistered yards. They are local and violent, and Chen's watch starves them out of the market. Smugglers, Kira's crews among them, live on the tax gap. The Enlightened are the organization that matters: they hold sump water, tithe food and medicine, and turn families who lost a child into a private army. Chen and the Forger are rivals for the lower city. Neither of them is a government that can take the wall.
*   **The Post-Betrayal Fracture (The Schism):**
    *   When Mercer’s true nature as an alien Simulacrum is exposed in Chapter 20, the resistance fractures violently:
        1.  **The Zealots of Terra:** Hardline human purists who declare that all modified biology—including chimeric supersoldiers like Vram—must be slaughtered alongside the aliens to keep human DNA pure.
        2.  **The Pragmatic Realists (Tsunari's Network):** Baseline survivors, rogue scientists, and defecting chimeric soldiers who realize that combining un-spliced human resilience with chimeric Arkuns is the only way to disable the terra-pylons and survive.
