# Format

## 7. Manuscript Formatting & Typographic Conventions

To maintain literary immersion and consistent cross-format presentation (Amazon KDP Print, EPUB3, and Web Readers), all prose across all volumes must adhere to the following typographic standards:

### 1. Inner Thoughts vs. Spoken Dialogue
*   **Inner Thoughts (Direct Internal Monologue):** Rendered in **pure italics only**, strictly **without quotation marks**.
    *   *Correct:* `*Not me,* I think, cloth already up over my mouth as the silt ticks against the grating.`
    *   *Incorrect:* `"Not me," I think...` or `*"Not me,"* I think...`
*   **Spoken Dialogue (Current & Remembered/Flashbacks):** Enclosed in **double quotation marks (`"..."`)** in standard roman font.
    *   Applies to all vocalized speech—whether spoken in-person, whispered, shouted, heard over comms, or **remembered in a flashback**.
    *   *Correct:* `"Fly or paint the rocks, 701!"`
    *   *Incorrect:* `*‘Fly or paint the rocks, 701!’*` or `*"Fly or paint the rocks, 701!"*`
    *   *Rule of Thumb:* If vocal cords vibrated or someone spoke the words, use double quotation marks in standard roman font. If it exists only as an unspoken thought inside a character's own consciousness, use pure italics without quotes.

### 2. Environmental & Combat Sounds (Audio Philosophy)
*   **Zero Comic-Book Onomatopoeia:** Standalone, ALL-CAPS phonetic sound effects on their own line (e.g. `*WAAAAAIL. WAAAAAIL.*`, `*CRACK.*`, `*CLANG.*`, `*SKRRRRR.*`, `*HISSSS.*`) are strictly prohibited. They disrupt the immersive literary tone and read like comic book captions.
*   **Sensory Prose Integration (Primary Standard):** Weave auditory events directly into kinetic, visceral narrative prose that describes the physical resonance, vibration, and consequences of the sound:
    *   *Instead of:* `*WAAAAAIL. WAAAAAIL. WAAAAAIL.*`
    *   *Write:* `Sirens tear through the toxic smog—three concussive wails that vibrate the concrete conduit around my ribs and shudder through my teeth.`
    *   *Instead of:* `*CRACK.*`
    *   *Write:* `The titanium port at the base of my skull discharges with an ear-splitting snap, shooting a violent pulse of electrical static directly into my brainstem.`
*   **Subtle Mechanical Cues:** When a distinct acoustic trigger or mechanical release is vital to a micro-action beat, weave it into the sentence in lowercase italics (e.g., *a muted click-hiss*, *a metallic snap*, *silt ticking on zinc*).

### 3. Console Telemetry & Neural Data
*   **Authentic Lowercase Terminal Syntax:** All console readouts, HUD data, and system outputs in the markdown source files must be written in **lowercase** (or standard sentence-case), never screaming all-caps.
*   **Monospace Code Blocks:** Multi-line system readouts, decryption feeds, and incoming neural telemetry broadcasts must be formatted using fenced ````console code blocks.
*   **Synaptic Compulsions:** Brief mental flashes or leash commands sent through the spinal siphon are woven into the sentence in lowercase italics (e.g., *searing a single conditioned imperative into my skull: obey*).
