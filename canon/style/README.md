# Style

* `voice.md`: how a sentence moves, the two points of view, the rule against a lecture, how long a thought runs, who may spend a hard word, and the shelf the book is written toward (talk, want, and a feeling that is allowed to last).
* `sensory.md`: touch, scent, sound, and the colors.
* `format.md`: thoughts, dialogue, sounds, and telemetry on the page.
* `glossary.md`: the names. Use these. Do not coin a second word for a thing that already has one.
